## Turrets (Vežičky)

Each team has its own; they shoot only at the enemy. Automatic, a turning barrel; the players do not
control them; they cannot be built or occupied. HP like the buggy (100), unlimited ammunition, half the
buggy's rate of fire. They have to be smashed before one can get to the flag.

## Destructible walls (Zničiteľné múry)

Only a little taller than the vehicles; the gyrocopter flies over them. Low durability (~like the
motorbike, 40 HP), then they fall apart. The motorbike cannot destroy them – its shots are too weak (the
wall ignores its damage).

## Mines (Míny)

Only the truck lays them, separate ammunition: 5 mines per truck. After it is laid it blinks in the team
colour (orange / teal) for ~3 s and is not active yet; after that it only shines and is live. It explodes
under any ground vehicle, your own too, and destroys it at once – even a truck. Not the gyrocopter (it
flies over).

## Simplify Map 01 (Zjednodušiť Map 01)

Waiting for a proposal.

## Bushes (Kríky)

Spread across the map. Cover: you can hide behind a bush, but you can shoot through it. When a buggy or a
truck drives through it, it collapses and the cover is gone. The motorbike passes through without
destroying it; the gyrocopter flies over. Turrets need a direct line of sight: they do not shoot at a
target behind a bush, so you can sneak up on them (to be tried).

## Translation notes

- "Vežičky" is the diminutive of "veže", towers: literally "little towers". The project's word is Turret,
  because "tower" already names each Base's corner towers and its water tower.
- "Zničiteľné múry" are destructible walls. The project calls them Flag Walls (reading, 2026-10-07),
  because they stand around the Flag and "wall" alone already names the Base walls.
- "obsadiť" is to occupy or seize; the developer's request says "captured".
- "kadencia" is the rate of fire.
- "Kladí" (Mines) is written so on the board; the standard form is "kladie", lays. The meaning is plain:
  only the truck lays them.
- "ostrá" (of a mine) is literally "sharp": live, armed.
- "spláskne" (Bushes) is written so on the board; the standard form is "splaskne", it goes flat or
  collapses.
- "Kryt" (Bushes) is cover in the plain sense. The bushes are not the project's Cover, which is never
  destroyed.
