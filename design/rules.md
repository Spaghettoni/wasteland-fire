## Objective

Two Players, each with their own Base: Base A and Base B on Map 01. Each Base holds one Flag. A Player wins the moment they deliver the opponent's Flag to their own Base. A Player loses the moment they lose their last Motorbike (see Destruction and respawn), because only a Motorbike can carry a Flag. Fuel and ammo are restocked only at the supply buildings, and every new Unit starts with both full (the comments; decided 2026-10-10 and built by stories 022 to 024; since story 023 Fuel is refilled in the depot's two zones and the Fuel Cans are gone, and until story 024 ammo comes back only with a new Unit); without Fuel nothing drives or flies (see Resources). One match, no rounds: the source plays a single match (zápas) with no best-of series. Our word for that one match is Round (decided 2026-10-01): a Round is the whole game from the first spawn to the win or the loss, and nothing else ends it.

## Tokens and the Garage

- Each Player has a stock of Tokens, one count per Unit type. The source's example: Motorbike 5, Buggy 3, Truck 2, Gyrocopter 2. The counts are set by the Map and each Map may differ; the example is an example, not a rule.
- At the start of the Round and after every destruction the Player picks any Unit type with Tokens left, and the Unit appears in the Garage at their Base. The game keeps running meanwhile: the other Player is never paused while someone chooses.
- A destroyed Unit = minus 1 Token of its type. The Token is taken at the destruction, not at the spawn, so the Unit in play is not yet subtracted from the stock.
- A type with 0 Tokens cannot be picked again in this Round.
- The loss in Token terms (reading of the source, 2026-10-01): the destruction of a Motorbike that leaves the Player's Motorbike stock at 0 ends the Round with that Player's loss. A Player is never stranded without a loss: with Motorbike Tokens left they can always pick a Motorbike, and without any they have already lost.
- A Map gives every Player at least 1 Motorbike Token; with 0 the Player could neither carry a Flag nor lose (reading of the source, 2026-10-01).
- The choice and the respawn delay (decided 2026-10-01): the delay runs from the destruction; the Player may choose during the countdown, and the Unit appears in the Garage once the Player has chosen and the delay has passed, whichever is later. At the Round start there is no delay: the first Unit appears as soon as it is chosen.
- What the screen shows (decided 2026-10-03, Story 008): the choice shows each type's Token count and dims a type with 0 Tokens, and the cursor skips it, so a Player with Motorbike Tokens left can always choose; the HUD shows the Player's Motorbike Tokens and counts the Unit in play, so it reads 5 while the first Motorbike drives, 4 once that one is destroyed and 1 on the last Motorbike; the Round-over screen names the winner, or says that nobody won after the double loss.
- A Map's stock is checked when the Round begins (decided 2026-10-03, Story 008): the Round does not begin, and says why with one error, when the Map gives no stock, a negative count, a count for a Unit type that does not exist, a Unit type without an id or two with the same one, or when the Unit types are not exactly one that can carry the Flag with at least 1 Token (the Motorbike; "exactly one" is this file's reading of the source's "the last Motorbike").

## Units

Four Units: the combat trio Buggy, Truck and Gyrocopter, which beat one another as rock, paper, scissors, and the Motorbike apart, purely for Flags. Starting values from the source, to be tuned by playing:

| Unit | HP | DMG | Speed m/s | Turn deg/s | Flies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Motorbike (motorka) | 40 | 5 | 22 | 220 | no |
| Buggy (bugina) | 100 | 12 | 18 | 180 | no |
| Truck (truck) | 220 | 25 | 10 | 70 | no |
| Gyrocopter (gyrokoptéra) | 70 | 15 | 15 | 120 | yes |

- Buggy beats Truck: it circles the Truck, which cannot turn and aim in time.
- Truck beats Gyrocopter: the Gyrocopter has few HP; the Truck downs it in a few hits.
- Gyrocopter beats Buggy: it escapes over cliffs and water, where the Buggy cannot go, and shoots from safety.
- Weapons: every Unit has one weapon that fires straight ahead, so turning is aiming and the turn rate is the aiming speed; there is no aim independent of the heading. Shooting happens at one height only: the Gyrocopter shoots and is shot like everyone else. This replaces the 2026-09-29 rule that only the Buggy's gun could hit the Gyrocopter.
- Ammo per type: every Unit's gun holds a load of ammo (the comments; see Resources), counted in Shots: each Shot spends one, and a full load is 20 s of continuous fire at the type's own rate, so Motorbike 100, Buggy 50, Truck 25 and Gyrocopter 40 Shots (reading, 2026-10-10; UnitStats data in Shots, where 0 means unlimited; the 20 s kept by the developer on 2026-10-14, story 022's question 1). The Turrets keep their unlimited ammunition (the board's rule, kept: reading, 2026-10-10; kept again by the developer on 2026-10-14, story 022's question 2). As built (story 022, 2026-10-14): `UnitStats.ammo_capacity` is a line of each of the four files (100, 50, 25 and 40, 0 meaning no count at all); the Weapon holds the count of a life, refills it to the type's load on the first tick it finds its Unit in play (the physics frame of the spawn, measured at the Round's first spawn, at respawns after each kind of destruction the build has and after Swaps), zeroes it when the Unit leaves play, spends one Shot for each Shot launched, and with none left fires nothing (it keeps the cadence of its last real Shot, as before); a load takes 1188, 1176, 1152 and 1170 ticks (19.8, 19.6, 19.2 and 19.5 s) from the first Shot to the last, 12, 24, 48 and 30 ticks apart; `Weapon.rearm(shots)` adds whole Shots through a carry for story 024.
- Turning on the spot (decided 2026-10-03, after the first playtest): every Unit turns on the spot when it stands, at its spot turn rate (`UnitStats.spot_turn_rate`; starting values half of each type's turn rate, to tune by playing), so a standing Unit can aim. A moving Unit never turns slower than that rate, so the turn does not die away as it drives off, and at speed it steers as before. In reverse every type turns at its spot rate, because none reverses fast enough to pass it (measured in story 009). A ground Unit with no Fuel still turns at its own empty turn rate.
- Flying means moving only in the high Height, never coming down into the low one (the comments; see Heights): the Gyrocopter crosses every low piece, the cliffs, the water, the cover and every Wall Panel, and passes over Mines without setting them off; every Tall piece stops it, and one it flies straight into fast enough destroys it (decided 2026-10-10). What it flies over grew in steps: the cover after the first playtest (decided 2026-10-03: the Gyrocopter flies over the wrecks, the containers and the scrap walls, while the depot's Fuel tanks and the Base walls still stop it); the Flag Walls and the Mines on 2026-10-07 (the board: the Gyrocopter flies over both), while the Turrets stop it like the Fuel tanks (reading, 2026-10-07); and on 2026-10-10 the Base walls and the Garage walls, which become Wall Panels, as the scrap walls do (decided 2026-10-10; stories 018 and 019). The Gyrocopter gains nothing else from the air: no height advantage for fire, no immunity from Shots (the Turrets fire at it too: reading, 2026-10-07), no extra reach, and the two Heights change none of that (decided 2026-10-10). Shots fly at the one shooting height and every hit volume spans both Heights, so a Gyrocopter over a piece of cover or a Wall Panel can neither shoot out of it nor be shot through it (measured in story 009 for the cover; decided 2026-10-10 for every Wall Panel). A Gyrocopter and a ground Unit never block each other: it passes through them and they through it (decided 2026-10-01), and two Gyrocopters pass through each other too (reading, 2026-10-10); so only the low pieces, the Mines and the crash into a Tall piece tell it apart.
- The Motorbike carries Flags; it still has its weak weapon, and the Carrier can shoot (decided 2026-09-29). Its Shots cannot break a Flag Wall: they are too weak, and the wall ignores their damage (the board), and since 2026-10-10 every Wall Panel ignores them too (decided 2026-10-10). They do damage a Turret (reading, 2026-10-07; kept by the developer on 2026-10-08, story 013's question 5).
- The Truck is the only Unit that lays Mines, as separate ammunition beside its gun: 5 per Truck (the board; see Turrets, Flag Walls and Mines).
- Damage multipliers. From the raw parameters alone the triangle easily collapses into a "best Unit", so a damage-multiplier matrix goes with them. Attacker in the rows, target in the columns:

| Attacker ↓ / target → | Buggy | Truck | Gyrocopter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buggy | 1.0 | 1.5 | 0.5 |
| Truck | 0.5 | 1.0 | 1.5 |
| Gyrocopter | 1.5 | 0.5 | 1.0 |

  The Motorbike deals and receives 1.0× against every Unit type. Damage taken = the attacker's DMG × the multiplier. While the matrix is in use its shape is fixed: above 1.0 against the Unit you beat, below 1.0 against the one that beats you, 1.0 against your own type and 1.0 for the Motorbike both ways. The two off-diagonal values are starting values. If the triangle shows even without the matrix, every multiplier is set to 1.0.
- Turrets and Flag Walls in the matrix (2026-10-07). They take damage through the same matrix, in rows keyed by their own type ids, never through branches in code: every Unit type deals 1.0 against a Turret, the Motorbike included (reading, 2026-10-07; kept by the developer on 2026-10-08, story 013's questions 1 and 5); every type but the Motorbike deals 1.0 against a Flag Wall (reading, 2026-10-07; kept by the developer on 2026-10-08, story 012's questions), and the Motorbike 0.0 (the board: the wall ignores its damage); a Turret's Shot deals 1.0 against every Unit type (reading, 2026-10-07; kept by the developer on 2026-10-08, story 013's question 1). Of these only the Motorbike's 0.0 against a Flag Wall needs a row in `src/gameplay/combat/data/damage_matrix.tres` (story 012); every other pair reads its 1.0 default (the script's own default is 0.0, so a new matrix file must set it). The 1.0 fallback above is about the triangle: the Motorbike's 0.0 against a Flag Wall stays (reading, 2026-10-07).
- Badly damaged (the comments: "By mohli mat state, ked maju napr 20% ze sa z nich dymi a idu pomalsie. O polovicu napr.", they could have a state: when they are at, for example, 20% [of their hit points], smoke comes out of them and they go slower. By half, for example): a Unit of any of the four types at or below 20% of its own maximum hit points (Motorbike 8, Buggy 20, Truck 44, Gyrocopter 14) is badly damaged: smoke rises from it and it moves at half its top speed (decided 2026-10-10: all four types; both numbers are the author's "for example", napr., so they are per-type data starting at 0.2 and 0.5). The state is read from the hit points and ends only with the Unit (destroyed, respawned or Swapped); it changes nothing but the top speed, forward and backward, eased in at the Unit's own rate, and the smoke: turning, the weapon, the Fuel burn and the HUD stay as they are, and in a Ford the lower cap wins (reading, 2026-10-10). The smoke's look is in Teams and visual style. Built in story 017 (2026-10-11); kept by the developer on 2026-10-12, story 017's questions 1, 2, 3 and 5.
- Data. The source gives each vehicle a VehicleStats resource (.tres) with max_hp, damage, speed, turn_rate, can_fly, fuel_use. Our resource is UnitStats (decided 2026-10-01): it already carries max_hit_points, max_speed, acceleration, braking, reverse_max_speed, turn_rate (stored in rad/s; the table's deg/s are converted when entered), can_carry and carry_offset; damage, can_fly and fuel_use are added beside them when the stories need them. The multiplier matrix is data too, never branches in code. Story 014 adds a Mines group to UnitStats: how many Mines a Unit of the type carries (5 for the Truck, 0 for the others) and where behind it a Mine is laid. The Turrets and the Flag Walls are not Units and get data resources of their own (stories 012 and 013). Since 2026-10-10 UnitStats also carries, per type: the fraction of the hit points at or below which a Unit is badly damaged and the share of its top speed it keeps then (0.2 and 0.5 for every type to start; decided 2026-10-10), the ammo a full load holds, in Shots (0 meaning unlimited; reading, 2026-10-10) and the crash speed at which a Unit that flies into a Tall piece is destroyed (4 m/s for the Gyrocopter to start, decided 2026-10-10; 0, never, for the ground types, reading, 2026-10-10).

## Heights

Comments 4 and 5 of 2026-10-10 give the game two heights (výškové úrovne): "tiez by som spravil, ako sme sa bavili, ze existuju 2 vyskove urovne - v 1. su vozidla, mury, balvany, ... - v 2. lieta gyro a su napr hlavy veziciek. Mozno niektore budovy budu aj v tej vyske. - zaroven hitboxy su vzdy v oboch - cez celu vysku." (I would also make it, as we talked about, so that there are 2 height levels: in the 1st are the vehicles, walls, boulders, ...; in the 2nd the gyro flies, and there are, for example, the turrets' heads. Maybe some buildings will be at that height too. At the same time the hitboxes are always in both, through the whole height.) and "a teda gyrokoptera sa pohybuje len v tej 2. urovni. vobec neklesa do prvej" (and so the gyrocopter moves only in that 2nd level; it never descends into the first at all). Comment 4 says the two were talked over before ("ako sme sa bavili", as we talked about); nothing of that talk is in the repository, so this section is built from the comments and the developer's answers of 2026-10-10. Our word is Height (výšková úroveň), low and high, and a piece that reaches the high Height is a Tall piece (comment 6's "high obstacle", vysoká prekážka): "level" is avoided because it would read as a Round or a Map, and "layer" because it means a physics layer (reading, 2026-10-10; kept by the developer on 2026-10-14, story 020's question 7; see `CONTEXT.md`). Story 020 builds the two Heights and story 021 the crash, after the Turrets grow in story 015 and every wall has become a Wall Panel in stories 018 and 019 (decided 2026-10-10).

- The two Heights (the comments): the low Height holds the ground Units and the pieces that stop only them, and the high Height is where the Gyrocopter flies and the Turrets' heads are. A low piece stops ground Units but not the Gyrocopter: the Wall Panels (the Flag Walls among them), the cover, the cliffs and the water (reading, 2026-10-10; kept by the developer on 2026-10-14, story 020's question 1). A Tall piece reaches the high Height and stops every Unit: the corner towers, the water towers (the legs and the tank), the depot's three Fuel tanks and the Turrets (decided 2026-10-10); the tank stands 8.3 m to 13.3 m up with no collider, above the flying Gyrocopter, so in play only the legs meet it (`src/gameplay/maps/map_01/compound_base.tscn:24-28`, `:356-359`; story 021's question 4, kept by the developer on 2026-10-14). The new ammunition buildings are low, so the Gyrocopter flies over them; the Fuel building is the depot, whose three Fuel tanks stay Tall pieces (decided 2026-10-10).
- The flying height (reading, 2026-10-10; kept by the developer on 2026-10-14, story 020's questions 2 to 4: 2.5 m with the Truck's antenna drawn through the gear, the Gyrocopter always up, two Gyrocopters passing through each other): only the Gyrocopter's model flies, at one constant height, its lowest point at about 2.5 m (the Truck's pod plus a clearance; its thin antenna is not counted; measured in story 015, set in story 020). - The crash (the comments: a destroyed vehicle explodes also when "gyrokoptera narazi do vysokej prekazky", the gyrocopter crashed into a high obstacle): a Gyrocopter that flies straight into a Tall piece faster than its crash speed (4 m/s to start, about a quarter of its top speed; data) is destroyed in that tick and explodes; a graze, a slow nudge or a turn on the spot against a Tall piece only stops it, as before. It is a destruction like any other: a Token is taken and the respawn wait runs (decided 2026-10-10). The test (reading, 2026-10-10; kept by the developer on 2026-10-14, story 021's questions 1 to 3): right after the Unit's move (`move_and_slide()`), a slide collision with a Tall piece whose arrival speed along the collision's normal, how fast the Unit flies straight into it, reaches the type's crash speed (UnitStats data; 0 means never, the ground types' value) destroys the Unit in that tick; the floor never counts; the invisible sea edge stops the Gyrocopter but never crashes it (its bodies are told apart by a group or metadata, not by a new physics layer); the Tall piece takes no damage. Story 021 builds it. As built (story 021, 2026-10-13): the crash speed is the type's `UnitStats.crash_speed`, in m/s, 4.0 in `gyrocopter_stats.tres` and no line, so 0, in the other three types' files (`first_problem()` refuses a negative one or a NaN); the arrival is the Unit's real velocity at the end of the previous tick along the collision's normal into the piece, never the speed it commands, so a Gyrocopter pinned against a piece with the throttle held (its command climbs to the top speed while it does not move) never crashes, and every spawn zeroes it, so a new Gyrocopter never arrives with its last life's motion; the speed is read along the normal whichever way the Unit travels, so a Gyrocopter backing tail first into a Tall piece at its full reverse speed (6 m/s, over the crash speed) crashes too (question 6 of the story, kept by the developer on 2026-10-14); the Floor is told apart by its normal against the up direction and the body's floor angle, which holds while a Map's slopes stay under that angle (the slope rule: 30 degrees or under); the sea edge's 32 bodies are in the group `map_edge`, stored in `map_01_terrain.tscn`, and stay on the map layer. The crash lands in the Gyrocopter's own tick, right after its move and before the Round's loss check: a crash on a Round's last tick costs its Token on that tick and changes no result, a Shot that arrives in the same tick finds the Gyrocopter gone and flies on (one a tick earlier destroys it first), a tank that empties on the tick of the contact destroys it by the Fuel, one tick short of the piece, and once the Round is over nothing moves, so nothing crashes. The measured edges are in the tuning list below.

## Resources

- Fuel (benzín). Every Unit burns Fuel, faster while it moves than while it stands (two rates per type, decided 2026-10-03 after the first playtest; the standing rate starts at a quarter of the moving one), the Gyrocopter the most; a Gyrocopter hovering still burns its standing rate. Without Fuel nothing drives or flies. Fuel is refilled only in the Fuel building's zones, and every new Unit starts with a full tank (the comments; see Supply buildings below and Destruction and respawn). The Fuel Cans (kanister) that respawned at fixed places on the Map are gone from Map 01, with their respawn and their restock at R (decided 2026-10-10; built in story 023 on 2026-10-14: the nine are off the Map and nothing gives Fuel but the depot's zones); the FuelCan class, its scene and its settings stay as evidence-only code, for the scenarios written before the story, because story 023 did not find them unused (reading, 2026-10-10: R19; `docs/tech-debt-register.md`, TD-015). Capacity, burn rates and the refill time are tuning values; the source gives no numbers for them.
- Ammo (the comments: "pridal  by som teda aj ammo okrem fuelu.", so I would also add ammo besides fuel). ## Destruction and respawn

- A destroyed Unit costs 1 Token of its type (see Tokens and the Garage). Every way of being destroyed counts: enemy fire, a Self-destruct, a Gyrocopter crash, and since 2026-10-07 a Turret's fire and a Mine, the Player's own Mine included (reading, 2026-10-07: the rule as it stands, a Turret's kill kept by the developer on 2026-10-08, story 013's question 6, and a Mine's, the Player's own included, on 2026-10-09, story 014's question 1; a carried Flag drops at the wreck as after any destruction). Since 2026-10-10 a Gyrocopter that flies into a Tall piece faster than its crash speed is destroyed too, a destruction like any other (the comments; decided 2026-10-10; see Heights); "a Gyrocopter crash" above is its crash with an empty tank.
- A destroyed Unit explodes (the comments: "bolo by fajn aby znicene vozidlo vybuchlo. ci uz je znicene, samoznicene, alebo gyrokoptera narazi do vysokej prekazky", it would be nice if a destroyed vehicle exploded, whether it was destroyed, self-destructed, or the gyrocopter crashed into a high obstacle). The explosion plays for every destruction that counts, an enemy Shot, a Turret's Shot, a Mine, a Self-destruct, the Gyrocopter's Fuel crash and its crash into a Tall piece, and never for a Swap or a bench; it is a picture only: it damages nothing near it and leaves no wreck behind (a carried Flag still drops where the Unit was destroyed). It lasts about 2 s, plays at the Unit's drawn model (a Gyrocopter's at its flying height) in both views, keeps playing under the Round-over screen, and is freed at R; a Mine keeps its own 0.3 s flash and the victim's explosion plays with it (reading, 2026-10-10, built so by story 016 and kept by the developer on 2026-10-11, story 016's questions 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6; its look is in Teams and visual style). As built (story 016, 2026-10-11): one listener on the Round's `unit_destroyed` signal (`SplitScreen._explode()`) hears every destruction the Round counted and nothing else: a Swap and every bench go through `Unit.leave_play()`, which emits nothing, and a destruction the controller refuses (a Player who is not alive, the one warning it logs) is announced by no signal, so none of them plays one; no cause is named anywhere, so the Gyrocopter's crash into a Tall piece (story 021) plays it with no change. The Explosion (`src/gameplay/effects/explosion.tscn`; its values are `src/gameplay/effects/data/explosion_settings.tres`) is added under World, where the Units are, in the tick of the destruction, and placed at the Unit's drawn Model raised by the height of the type's `collision_center`: a ground Unit's at the middle of its box, a Gyrocopter's where it was seen in the air (3.0 m above the floor since story 020: its model is drawn from 2.5 m to 4.3 m), the Unit's own origin when it has no model; its size is the larger horizontal side of the type's box. It holds no body and no Area3D, so a Unit drives through it, a Shot flies through it and a dropped Flag is picked up in it, and it stays where it began. It counts its own physics ticks and frees itself 120 ticks (2.0 s) after the tick it was added in (its first own tick is the next one; measured), before the destroyed Unit can respawn (3 s). Its process mode is ALWAYS, so the explosion that ends a Round plays out under the Round-over screen (measured on 4.7.2: an ALWAYS node's `_physics_process` runs on every physics frame of the pause and its pieces are drawn interpolated at 120 fps), and `SplitScreen` frees every one still playing when a Round starts again (R). A Mine kill shows the Mine's own flash and the explosion in the same tick; the Mine is unchanged.
- The next Unit is chosen from the remaining stock and appears in the Garage at the Player's Base after a delay of about 3 s (decided 2026-09-29; the source gives no delay; tuning value, `MatchRules.respawn_delay_seconds`).
- Self-destruct (decided 2026-09-29): every Unit has an action that destroys it on purpose, so a stranded Player can get a new Unit. It costs a Token like any destruction (decided 2026-10-01), except inside the own Base, where it is the swap (below).
- Running out of Fuel (decided 2026-09-29): a ground Unit with no Fuel stops but can still turn and fire. A Gyrocopter with no Fuel crashes and counts as destroyed. A Player whose ground Unit has run out is shown how to Self-destruct, with their own key, until the Unit is refuelled or gone or the Round ends (decided 2026-10-03, after the first playtest); inside the own Base the hint says to swap the Unit instead (story 011: "Out of Fuel! ## Camera and controls

The source leaves this open for a test; v0.1 skips the test (decided 2026-10-01).

- The open question as the source states it. Camera: fixed vs turning with the vehicle. Return Fire had a fixed camera; the author is considering a camera that turns with the vehicle. Prepare both and play them in split screen. The camera is a separate node (PlayerCamera) that follows the vehicle, not a child of it, with the exports `follow_rotation: bool` (whether the camera takes the vehicle's heading), `rotation_smoothing: float` (so the camera does not turn with every small movement), `look_ahead: float` (the view shifted forward in the direction of travel) and `pitch_degrees: float` (90 = straight down, about 60 = angled from behind the vehicle). The settings may differ per Unit (the Gyrocopter is perhaps better with a fixed camera). With a turning camera the author expects VEHICLE_RELATIVE to be better, with a fixed one SCREEN_RELATIVE; to verify. If the turning camera wins, v0.1 also needs an arrow to your own Base or a minimap (north up). After the test, write down the decision and shorten the section.

## Technology and conventions

The source's choices, and how the project already matches them:

- Godot 4.x, exact version to be written down (the source leaves the blank): the project pins Godot 4.7.2 (`project.yaml`, `docs/engine-reference/godot/VERSION.md`). GDScript with types (the source's example: `var speed: float = 10.0`).
- Split screen via SubViewportContainer + SubViewport, one camera per Player: built in story 002.
- Input through the Input Map with a Player prefix. The source's examples are p1_accelerate and p2_fire; the project's actions are p1_throttle, p1_reverse, p1_steer_left, p1_steer_right, p1_self_destruct and the p2_ set (stories 002 and 003); a fire action joins them with the weapons. p1_lay_mine on E and p2_lay_mine on Comma join them with the Truck's Mines (story 014; reading, 2026-10-07; kept by the developer on 2026-10-09, question 7, with the five lay-key combinations confirmed on the real keyboard).
- Folder layout (decided 2026-10-01): the source's scenes/ (one scene = one thing: unit_bike.tscn, pickup_fuel.tscn, level_01.tscn), scripts/ (mirrors scenes/), assets/ (models, textures, sounds) and docs/ (backlog.md, decisions) is the author's layout and is not adopted. Ours stays: src/gameplay, src/ui, tools/, production/, the CCGS structure.
- VehicleStats is UnitStats here (decided 2026-10-01); see Units.
- File and variable names in English, snake_case; classes PascalCase with class_name: the `naming` block of `project.yaml` says the same.
- Tuning values (speed, consumption, HP, dmg) as @export, not hardcoded: ours live in `.tres` resources (UnitStats, MatchRules) whose fields are @export.
- Communication between objects through signals, not by searching the tree for nodes: the match controller already works by signals (stories 003 and 004).

## Process rules
