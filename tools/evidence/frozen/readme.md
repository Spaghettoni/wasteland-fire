# Frozen scenes (evidence only)

Nothing under `src/` uses a file here. The evidence runner (`tools/evidence/split_screen_harness.gd`)
swaps these in for the scenarios written before the story that changed the shipped piece, so their
retained outputs stay what they were measured with.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `turret_story_013.tscn` | The Turret of Story 013 as `main` had it at 2f59a2c, before Story 015: a drum 1 m tall under a head, a collider 1.8 m tall, no damaged look, and the map art's trim on the drum (`TurretPedestalTrim`) and on the head. The runner puts one in each Turret place of a Map Base for every scenario that does not declare `TURRET_LOOKS` (`_apply_turret_looks_off()`). It runs the shipped `turret.gd` and `structure.gd`, which are inert on it: its `intact` is `Look`, it names no worn parts, and the runner empties the stats' `damaged_below`. |
| `base_walls_story_007.tscn` | The five Base walls of Story 007 as `main` had them at 03ed864, before Story 018: 3.2 m StaticBody3D on the map layer, with the map art's trims and the wall material. The runner puts them back on every Map Base for every scenario that does not declare `WALL_PANELS` (`_apply_wall_panels_off()`). |
| `garage_walls_story_007.tscn` | The three Garage walls of Story 007 as `main` had them at 03ed864, before Story 019: 2.5 m StaticBody3D on the map layer (the default layer 1), with the map art's trims (`GarageBackTrim`, `GarageSideTrim`) and the wall material. The runner puts them back on every Map Base for every scenario that does not declare `GARAGE_SCRAP_PANELS` (`_apply_garage_panels_off()`). |
| `scrap_walls_story_007.tscn` | The two scrap walls of Story 007 as `main` had them at 03ed864, before Story 019: two 2.5 x 2 x 12 m StaticBody3D on the cover layer, drawn by the props' `ScrapWall` mesh. The runner puts them back under `Cover/ScrapWalls` for every scenario that does not declare `GARAGE_SCRAP_PANELS`, in place of the Map's `FieldWalls` (`_apply_field_panels_off()`), before the pre-009 Map gives the cover its old layer. |

| `gyrocopter_model_story_019.tscn` | The Gyrocopter's model scene as stories 007 to 019 left it (the file is the one `main` has at 03ed864, which stories 018 and 019 did not touch): `hover_height` 0.78 m, the rise over the cliffs and the cover (`rise_over_cliffs`, `rise_mask` 32, `rise_body_mask` 64, `rise_clearance` 0.25, a rise of 6 and a settle of 2 m/s, a lead of 0.4 s up to 8 m, a margin of 2 m) and story 017's smoke export. The runner gives it to every UnitStats of `match_rules.tres` whose model is the shipped scene for every scenario that does not declare `HEIGHTS` (`_apply_heights_off()`), after the greybox models, so that the older scenarios keep the Gyrocopter they were measured with; its rise is the code of `unit_model.gd` that no shipped scene uses any more. |
| `fuel_cans_story_022.tscn` | The nine Fuel Cans of Map 01 as stories 007 to 022 left them: four as the Map's children right after `Depot` (`FuelCanCanyonWest`, `FuelCanCanyonEast`, `FuelCanFlatWest`, `FuelCanFlatEast`) and five as the depot's last children (`FuelCanDepot1` to `5`), each an instance of `src/gameplay/fuel/fuel_can.tscn` at the place and under the name the two Map scenes stored at 03ed864 (the Can scene, its script and its settings stay under `src/` as evidence-only code: TD-015). The runner removes every Fuel `SupplyZone` of the Map (the ammunition buildings, which story 024 added, are removed before it: `_apply_ammo_buildings_off()`, no frozen scene needed) and puts these back, in their old order and with their old owners, for every scenario that does not declare `FUEL_BUILDING` (`_apply_fuel_building_off()`); a scenario on the greybox field keeps its own five Cans. |

| `wasteland_gyrocopter_story_024.glb` | The Gyrocopter's mesh file as `main` had it at 14eec71, before Story 025: the Accent and the Neutral and nothing that turns (the rotor's blades and tips are part of the Accent, its hub of the Neutral, the propeller's blades, hub and spinner of the Neutral and its tips of the Accent), a byte copy (`git show 14eec71:assets/art/vehicles/wasteland_gyrocopter/wasteland_gyrocopter.glb`, md5 3499714fba6af15fa07d7bdfeb0acfd3), with its `.import` written by the engine's import. The shipped file is rebuilt with six meshes (the body's Accent and Neutral and RotorAccent, RotorNeutral, PropellerAccent and PropellerNeutral) holding the same 13,231 vertices, positions, normals and colours. The runner gives it to every UnitStats of `match_rules.tres` whose model is the shipped Gyrocopter scene or the frozen scene of Story 019 for every scenario that does not declare `ROTORS` (`_apply_rotors_off()`, after `_apply_heights_off()`): it packs an instance of the scene with `mesh_scene` set to this file and `rotor_settings` null (`_without_rotors()`), so the model adds no pivot and no smear and draws the Accent and the Neutral as it did. Both model scenes name the shipped file, and a frozen scene is never edited, so the swap is made on the instance. |
| `player_hud_story_032.tscn` | Each Player's HUD as `main` had it at b1f9ee1, before Story 036: the hit-point bar and line, the Fuel bar with its "Fuel n / max" line at x 332 to 624, the status and Tokens lines; no icons, no ammo bar. A byte copy (`git show b1f9ee1:src/ui/hud/player_hud.tscn`); it runs the shipped `player_hud.gd`, whose Fuel line and ammo bar are optional. The runner puts one in place of each PlayerHud for every scenario that does not declare `RESOURCE_BARS`, and puts the story 022 Ammo line (`src/ui/hud/ammo_count.tscn`, which the launch scene no longer holds) back for those that declare `AMMO` (`_apply_resource_bars_off()`). |

| `map_01_story_054.tscn`, `compound_base_story_054.tscn`, `map_01_cover_story_054.tscn` | Map 01, its compound Base and its cover as `main` had them at 5601089, before Story 058: the four 6 m corner towers, the unbreakable wrecks and containers, the ford's own rectangular water. Byte copies, except that the Map's two ext_resource paths of the compound and the cover point at the copies here. The runner plays this Map for every scenario that names no `MAP_SCENE` and does not declare `OPEN_BASES` (`_apply_map_scene()`). |

A frozen scene is a byte copy (`git show <commit>:<path>`): never edit one to follow the shipped
piece. When a later story changes what the older scenarios would see, add a new frozen copy.
