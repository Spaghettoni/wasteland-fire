## Beyond the story's list

1. **A latent flaw in `base_walls_gate.gd` found and fixed by the new modules:** its last check (`lock`) left the raider carrying Base B's Flag and its closing `restart()` pressed R in a Round that was still on, so the next module began with a Carrier that the first teleport into its own Garage delivered: the Round ended and the world froze, in which a Turret that "does not fire" proves nothing. `lock` now delivers first, and `base_walls` carries `modules_leave_a_fresh_round`, which fails when a module ends with the Round over or a Flag carried.
2. **Count guards:** a typed-array initialiser with a ternary inside printed a script error that aborted two functions of `base_walls_mines.gd`, and the verdict still read PASS over zero readings; the check now asserts how many readings, probe readings, refusals and lays were made (296, 24, 16 and 4).
3. **The panel scenes are generated** by `tools/wall_panels/make_wall_panel.py` (three scenes, one per run length, the Flag Wall's own look nodes), whose `--self-test` reproduces `flag_wall.tscn` byte for byte; the story left the build's choice open.
4. **The old walls' trim is gone:** the panels carry no Blender trim (the old walls' posts, cap and wire were modelled for 3.2 m walls); the frozen old walls keep theirs for the older scenarios. See the question for the author.
5. **The showcase records the action moments in both views,** since the corner tower hides a Truck in the breach beside it in the chase view (frame 09 is the view from above).
