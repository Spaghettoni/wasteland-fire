## Notes, gaps and decisions recorded here

- **"Moving" reads the drive speed**: a Unit burns while its drive speed is not approximately zero, so a Unit pinned against a wall with the throttle held keeps burning (its engine runs), a coasting Unit burns until it stands and a hovering Gyrocopter burns nothing (decision log 2026-10-02).
- **A Fuel Can is taken only by a Unit with room**, so a Unit standing with a full tank leaves it. A Unit that drives burns every tick and always has a little room: a full Motorbike driving onto a Can from 8, 30 and 60 m gains 1.47, 3.50 and 6.00 of the 40, and the Can is gone for 20 s (`measurements/can-nearly-full-driver-probe-out.txt`). This is the design as written; whether a Can should need a minimum room is a balance question for the author (human check below).
- **The pause counts toward a Can's delay** (a frame stamp keeps counting while the tree is paused); the only pause in v0.1 is the Round-over screen, after which every Can is restocked.
- **Starting values** (to tune by playing): capacity 100 for every type, `fuel_use` 2.0 / 2.5 / 3.0 / 5.0, spawn share 0.5, empty turn 1.4 / 1.15 / 0.45 rad/s, a Can gives 40 and comes back after 20 s. On the 80 m greybox field a fresh tank lasts 25 s of motion for the Motorbike and 10 s for the Gyrocopter; Map 01 (story 007) is 300 m long.
- **The Fuel Cans' amber and the gauge's amber** are close to Player 1's coming Orange (the architecture review): story 007 recolours them as data.
- **Sizes**: `unit.gd` grew by 122 lines to 687 (TD-010); the three scenarios are over the size rule (TD-006).
