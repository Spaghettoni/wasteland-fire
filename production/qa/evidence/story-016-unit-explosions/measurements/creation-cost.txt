## Reading
The destruction itself costs a frame of 10 to 17 ms (median about 14.5) with no explosion at all: the controller's bookkeeping, the HUD,
the choice panel and the respawn countdown in both views; nothing here itemizes it. The explosions add about 1.5 ms for each one made
in that tick: three at once measure 10 to 20 ms in the retained runs (median about 18; 22 in an earlier run), the Round's first
explosions 19.1 and 20.1 ms. The Round's first explosion measured 35.8 ms before the warm-up (the first instantiate of a run costs
12 to 16 ms) and 17.4 ms with it. Steady frames are 4 to 12 ms at 118 to 120 fps with at most 275 draw calls (265 with no Unit in play, 274
in the control window with both Trucks standing). The bar of the story (a frame of at most 16.6 ms) is met by every frame but the one a
destruction is made in, which it misses by up to about 4 ms with three explosions in it, the larger part of it the destruction's own work.
