## Measured facts the story asked for

1. **The tick the Weapon first sees a new Unit** (Measure first 1): the same physics frame as the spawn, at the Round's first spawn, after every respawn and every Swap (`report_lag=0,0` in the RESULT; `judge_new_unit` pins it at every new Unit). A trigger held by the Weapon's own call fires in the spawn frame itself, from the refilled load (`first_tick_fires_from_the_load`).
2. **The cadence** (Measure first 2): 12, 24, 48 and 30 ticks, the last Shot 1188, 1176, 1152 and 1170 ticks after the first, as the story computed.
3. **Whether any older output fires past a load** (Measure first 3): two do, `base_walls` and `garage_scrap_walls` (the strip probes above).
4. **The Ammo line's rectangle** (Measure first 4): x 16 to 316 and y 92 to 120 of the 640 x 720 view (`ammo_line=(16,92:300x28)`), 8 px under the HUD band (bottom 84), on the Mines line's row (332,92 292x28) with 16 px between, 8 px above the notice line (40,128 560x50), 474 px from the out-of-Fuel hint (40,594 560x50), clear of the choice panel (40,536 560x168) and of the respawn countdown (112,312 415x96); its widest text "Ammo 100 / 100" is 162 px wide on one line in a label 300 px wide.
5. **The notice**: 90 ticks (1.5 s) each time; a second press 45 ticks into it restarts the time (137 ticks in all, the press taking its two ticks).
6. **`rearm(shots)`**: an empty Motorbike rearmed with 0.4167 a call (25 Shots a second) holds the whole Shots of n x 25 / 60 after every call n, is full at call 240, and reports once for each call that added a Shot; ten tenths of a Shot make one (the float sum is 0.9999999999999999, which the tolerance covers); 25 / 60 itself sums cleanly, so only the tenths tell the tolerance.

Found while building, kept in the code and here:

- **The Weapon keeps its cadence across lives**, as it did before the story: a Buggy that fires its last Shot and is swapped 15 ticks later cannot fire its replacement's first Shot until 24 ticks have passed since that Shot. The refusals of an empty gun stamp nothing, so they never hold a new Unit back, but the cadence of the last real Shot still counts (the Weapon's class doc says so now); `first_tick_fires_from_the_load` waits that cadence out, and a seeded defect that stamps while refusing at zero is killed by it.
- **Two older lints print counts of code lines** of the HUD files this story extended (`mines_lint` and `tokens_data`'s T33), a printed dependency the story's grep of `.gd:N` lines did not list; both are pinned as exact rewrites computed from the source.
- A `Vector2` holds 32-bit floats: the window 19.2 to 19.8 s held as a `Vector2` made 19.8 compare greater than its own limit (the checks keep two 64-bit floats); `%.0e` is not a GDScript format; a local named `call` shadows `Object.call()`.
