#!/bin/sh
# The far scene's second set (the faster camera): frames 1121 to 1124 of rec/far26probe, each Player's far marks cropped at 3x
# (Player 1: 100,255 440x100; Player 2: 840,245 420x100), stacked.
S=/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-tomas-kunzo-projects-ronin-ya-wasteland-fire/903467d6-8576-4a19-ad15-5c4b7052d4e0/scratchpad
REC=$S/rec/far26probe
T=$S/far26
cd /Users/tomas.kunzo/projects/ronin-ya/wasteland-fire || exit 9
C="godot --headless --path . -s $S/lib/compose.gd --"
for n in 1121 1122 1123 1124; do
  $C crop $T/f1_$n.png $REC/f0000$n.png 100 255 440 100 3 > /dev/null 2>&1
  $C crop $T/f2_$n.png $REC/f0000$n.png 840 245 420 100 3 > /dev/null 2>&1
done
for p in f1 f2; do
  $C pair $T/${p}_a.png $T/${p}_1121.png $T/${p}_1122.png > /dev/null 2>&1
  $C pair $T/${p}_b.png $T/${p}_1123.png $T/${p}_1124.png > /dev/null 2>&1
  $C pair $T/${p}_all.png $T/${p}_a.png $T/${p}_b.png > /dev/null 2>&1
done
