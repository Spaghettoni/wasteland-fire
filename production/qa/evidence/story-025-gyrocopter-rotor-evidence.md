## Measured facts (the story's "Measure first")

1. **The mesh file.** The story said the file holds three meshes (Accent, Neutral, Lamp); the Gyrocopter's held two, Accent 1,296 and Neutral 6,106 triangles. The rebuilt file holds six: Accent 988, Neutral 5,876, RotorAccent 176, RotorNeutral 44, PropellerAccent 132 and PropellerNeutral 186 triangles, 7,402 in all as before. Its 13,231 vertices (13,199 distinct) are the old file's, one for one, with the same normals and the same vertex colours as the `.glb` holds them. `MeshUnitModel.hull_of()` reads from both files, to the bit, `[P: (-2.116208, -0.0, -1.16), S: (4.232415, 1.801629, 2.419473)]` at `INF` (raw bytes `f26f07c0000080b1e17a94bff26f0740c49be63f6736a13f`) and `[P: (-0.69, -0.0, -1.16), S: (1.38, 1.388867, 2.419473)]` at 1.4 (`d7a330bf000080b1e17a94bfd7a3303f68c6b13f6736a13f`). The shipped file's md5 is `9ca6ec05ee742e87499ce6833f05d0fb`, and the final scripts rebuild it byte for byte (`measurements/blender/gyrocopter-rebuild-final-check.txt`); an unchanged script rebuilds the file git has byte for byte (`3499714fba6af15fa07d7bdfeb0acfd3`), and the Truck, Motorbike, Buggy, props, base and rocks files, the six other models the kit builds, rebuild byte-identical to git's after the kit change (`measurements/blender/other-models-identical.txt`, md5 of each).
2. **The step.** The rotor turns 24 degrees a tick (4 turns a second over 60 ticks), 0.1333 of the 180 degrees between its two blades; the propeller 12 degrees a tick standing (0.1000 of the 120 degrees between its three blades) to 30 degrees at full speed (0.2500); the strobe share is 0.35, so the settings' limits are 10.50 turns a second for the rotor and 7.00 for the propeller.
3. **The first tick.** A new Gyrocopter is first drawn with its parts at rest: its model is added inside a physics tick and its first physics tick is the next one, so each life starts at angle 0 and the same frames follow (`out_of_play`: the rotor at 0.0 steps on its first drawn tick after a destruction and after a Swap).
4. **The swept band** (over a full turn, from the file's vertices: `band`). The main rotor's blades sweep **1.4263 to 1.9326 m** in the file's frame (the Blender script's tilt of 6 degrees and coning of 2.5 degrees give 1.4396 and 1.9200 by the story's formula; at rest the top was 1.8016 m), so 3.9263 to 4.4326 m in the world at the 2.50 m flying height; the Gyrocopter's whole drawn band over a turn is 2.5000 to 4.4326 m against 2.5000 to 4.3016 m at rest. The lowest water tower platform is 8.0000 m up: **3.5674 m over the swept rotor's top**. The propeller sweeps 0.3985 to 1.4015 m in the file's frame (2.8985 to 3.9015 m in the world).
5. **The sweep against the legs and the frame** (`sweep`). Between a water tower's legs (inner faces 1.700 m from its axis) the rotor's blades reach **0.4162 m into the legs at rest** (the 0.416 m of the story of the Heights, now reproduced from the file) and **0.5995 m over a full turn** (the blades' tips reach 2.2995 m from the hub). Along the axis the rotor's blades stay 0.4358 m clear of the frame (the fin is the nearest) and 0.1319 m clear of the propeller's tips. The propeller's blades are 0.0230 m over the keel (the script's numbers: about 3 cm) and 0.0156 m clear of the rest of the frame (nearest point (0.19838, 0.608704, 0.60338), the exhaust), and **0.0120 m into the mast brace**, a tube 4.8 cm thick that crosses the propeller's plane under the hub: the lowest tip of the turn grazes it at (-0.004412, 0.416792, 0.629985).
6. **What turns and what does not.** The explosion's centre is the Unit's place raised by the model's hover and the box's collision centre, 3.000 m, to the millimetre, for both Players (read from the model's own origin, not from what is drawn under it); a badly damaged Gyrocopter's smoke stands at the top centre of the hull of the file below its rotor, (0.0, 1.388867, 0.049736) in the file's frame and (-30.04974, 3.888867, -16.0) in the world, while its parts turn. A Round that ends stops the parts with everything else that counts ticks (45 ticks later the angles are the same, 5.8643 and 6.0737 rad) and the next Round's Gyrocopters start at rest.
7. **The speed share.** The propeller's share is the real speed along its facing over the type's top speed (16.40 m/s forward, 6.00 m/s in reverse), always the type's own top speed: a badly damaged Gyrocopter, whose top speed is half, settles at 8.20 m/s and so turns its propeller at 3.500 turns a second (half way); its rotor keeps 4.0.
8. **The importer.** Godot's import stores every mesh's vertex positions except the Neutral mesh's as 16-bit values over the mesh's own box, so the imported positions of the Accent and the four turning meshes differ from the `.glb`'s by up to 6.5e-5 m (Neutral exact); `hull_of()` reads the imported meshes and is bit-identical regardless. The scenario therefore checks vertex identity on the raw `.glb` data and the rest pose against the imported meshes within that rounding.
9. **The first Blender build.** The story's note suggested explicit `groups` in `finish()`. Built that way (a plain join of the turning parts into objects of their own) the occlusion bake ran over a different set and changed 36 to 40% of the vertex colours, by up to 0.0875 on the Accent and 0.0488 on the Neutral mesh: the older outputs measured the old colours' look. The build that shipped splits the tagged parts off **after** the join, bake and previews and before the origins (`SPLIT_LAYER`, `_tag_split()` and `_split_off()` in `tools/blender/wasteland_kit.py`, the parts tagged `Rotor` and `Propeller` in `build_gyrocopter.py`): item 1 is the result.

## The frame rate
