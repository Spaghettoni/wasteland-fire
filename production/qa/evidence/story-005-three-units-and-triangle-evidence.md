The checks as they were put:

1. **Choose and fire on the real keyboard, both Players at once.** Player 1 chooses with A / D and confirms with Space, Player 2 with Left / Right and Period; fire with the same keys once in play. At the start, after a destruction and after R. Does it feel right, and does a confirm EVER fire a stray shot (the arming should make that impossible: the probe shows 0 shots)? Is Period a good key for Player 2 (Right Shift cannot be used by the tools; the binding is data in `project.godot`)?
2. **Ghosting on your keyboard.** Run `godot --path . res://tools/evidence/input_ghosting_check.tscn` windowed and press the eight combinations, now including the two with fire (throttle + left + right + fire per Player); the self-test passes 8 of 8, your keyboard may not.
3. **The look.** Are the four Units readable and different enough in the chase camera (Motorbike, Buggy with its cage, Truck, Gyrocopter hovering with its rotor)? The choice panel: size, legibility, where it sits over the field. The tracers: easy enough to see?
4. **The Truck in the chase camera.** It is the biggest Unit; does it hide too much of the view? (Camera settings per Unit belong to the camera test after v0.1.)
5. **The feel of the triangle.** Fire rates, ranges, damage and speeds are starting values, all data (`src/gameplay/units/data/*.tres`, `combat/data/damage_matrix.tres`). One Truck hit takes 25 of the Motorbike's 40 hit points; a Buggy beats a Truck at 1.5, loses at 0.5; the Motorbike is the fastest and most agile. Does the triangle work between two people?
6. **The Gyrocopter** over the water and the cliff block: does crossing them feel like an advantage, and is passing through ground Units acceptable (decided 2026-10-01)?
7. **One key for fire and confirm.** The reviewer pointed out a consequence: when your Unit is destroyed while you fire or hold fire, the next press of the fire key confirms the type under the cursor at once, and a choice stands once made. Is that acceptable, or should the confirm get its own key?
