## Notes, gaps and decisions recorded here

- **A respawn onto a parked Unit (policy decided by the orchestrator, open to the designer).** Not in the story: the preflight measured that a Unit spawned inside another one ends under the floor or in the air, which the first sofa playtest (Story 004) would hit as soon as one Player parks on the other's spawn point. The workflow's fix rounds built a "wait up to 2 s, then use a spare" policy with a third rules value; the orchestrator simplified it to: on the due tick the Unit goes to the spawn point if free, else to the first free of the Base's two spare spawn points (4 m left and right), never inside a Unit; only with every spot taken does it keep waiting, tick by tick, and the label then reads "Respawn in 1". So AC-3's delay stays exactly 180 ticks in every measured case. `destruction` checks `blocked_spawn` (three blocker offsets, respawn on the 180th tick on spare 0, 4.00–4.30 m from the blocker) and `blocked_waits` (spares removed and the spawn point blocked: no spawn for 90 ticks past due, the wait reads one tick, the label "Respawn in 1", respawn 1 tick after the blocker leaves). Change the policy in one place, `MatchController._try_respawn()`.
- **The respawn clock is physics ticks, not delta time.** `.claude/rules/gameplay-code.md` asks for delta time in time-dependent calculations; the frame stamp is the frame-rate-independent clock that is also exact from every call context (table above). `Engine.time_scale` is not used by this game and is untested. The controller pushes every running wait on by the length of a pause (`NOTIFICATION_PAUSED` / `UNPAUSED`, tested by the `paused_wait` check); a wait already due and blocked is not pushed on.
- **Beacon on the front-right corner**, not the back edge the design said (reason and renders in the table above). Decision log.
- **The loose start markers stay** (`PlayerStart`, `Player2Start`), each Base's `spawn_point` pointing at them, so each Base's place is stated twice: **TD-005**, to pay in Story 007 when the Map replaces the field and the Story 001 harness may change. `MatchController.begin()` refuses a Round while a spawn point lies off its pad, so the duplication cannot drift silently.
- **Tech debt:** TD-003 resolved (the Story 002 harness is a 330-line runner plus one script per scenario, outputs byte-identical); **TD-006** registers the size of the scenario set (3,238 lines across 21 scripts, 36 checks for this story alone). Preload constants that hold scripts are PascalCase under `tools/` (the GDScript style-guide exception; decision log).
- **Hand-off for Story 004 (architecture lens):** the Water Canister can drop at `Unit.destroyed`, the Unit is still at the wreck when it fires; a HUD hit-point display can poll `hit_points` and `stats.max_hit_points` (a `hit_points_changed` signal is three emit lines if wanted); the Round-over restart is `get_tree().reload_current_scene()` or a short `restart()` that calls `_spawn()` per Player (`begin()` refuses a second call on purpose); the Base `Zone` (layer zones, mask units) sees both Players' Units and carries no Player index, so the owner check belongs in the controller. **For Story 005:** `spawn()` will take a `UnitStats` argument and the `_ready()`-only work (snap length, wall angle, saved layer and mask, `_can_drive`) must become callable from it; `PlayerDriveInput` turns itself off for good when its Unit is null at ready; setting `debug_damage` to 0 switches the debug keys off, and the action may then be deleted.
- **`.claude/rules/ui-code.md` not met in full:** scalable text and colourblind modes are mandatory there, and no settings system exists yet; the countdown's size, colour and outline are theme overrides stored in the scene so a text-scale setting can drive them later. Flagged for the UX designer; not built here.
- **Observations, not defects:** the first destruction of a session costs one 13–16 ms frame (the countdown's first appearance; every later respawn stays under 10.7 ms); the Unit on its pad shares the pad's colour and reads as a slightly darker block with the cream nose stripe (accepted; a second shade is a one-line data change); two Units start 40 m apart in one lane facing each other, so both Players holding throttle from the first second meet nose to nose after about 1.6 s (unchanged from Story 002; the Bases decide the start layout from here on).
- **Measurements of the first build still quoted here** (streak probes, mutation testing, fps, 16-cycle soak) were taken before the policy simplification; the simplification changed only which transform a due respawn chooses, not `spawn()`, the camera snap, the countdown or the input path, and every scenario was re-run on the final code (outputs retained beside the screenshots).
- **Out of this changeset:** `assets/art/**` (separate work).
