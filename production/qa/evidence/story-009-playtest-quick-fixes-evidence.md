## Stories 001 to 008 regression

Baselines of all 38 retained scenario outputs (the drive harness's four and 34 of the split-screen runner's; `fps` and `map_fps` are windowed tools) were captured at `ad5bb45` with the working tree clean, before the first edit. On the final code: 37 byte-identical, and `tokens_data` identical after two exact rewrites (`measurements/normalizers.json`): its lint of `src/gameplay/match` and `src/ui/hud` now reads 13 files and 1,194 code lines instead of 12 and 1,143, because the new hint script is in `src/ui/hud`; its count of integer literals is still 5. The older scenarios keep their outputs because the runner gives them the data they were measured with: spot turn rate and idle Fuel rate 0, the cover layer taken out of every mask, the Shot mask 19 and, on Map 01, the cover back on the map layer and the tanks' colliders freed (`split_screen_harness.gd`, `_apply_pre_009_data()` and `_apply_pre_009_map()`); the drive harness sets the driving toy's spot turn rate to 0 the same way. That the old outputs stay byte-identical with the new code is the proof that every new branch is inert with its data at zero (the turn and the burn are written so that a zero rate computes exactly the old expression).

The first full regression run had one failure that did not repeat: `loss` T24 counted an engine warning, "Jolt Physics job system exceeded the maximum number of jobs. This should not happen. Please report this. Waiting for jobs to become available...", once, while another Godot window was open on the machine (`measurements/regress-first-run-with-jolt-warning.txt`). `loss` then passed byte-identical in two runs on its own, and every later full run was clean. Recorded as an engine warning under load, not a defect of the story.

## Notes for the author

1. **Reversing turns faster than before.** No type reverses fast enough for its speed-scaled turn to pass its spot rate, so in reverse every Unit now turns at its spot rate, and the turn swings the nose the car way (steer left while backing up and the nose goes right) from the first tick of rolling back. Before, the turn grew from nothing with the reverse speed.
2. **A Gyrocopter over cover is in cover for fire.** Shots fly at the one shooting height, so it can neither shoot out of a piece it is over nor be shot through it. That follows from "cover stops every Shot" and "everyone shoots at one height"; say if it should be otherwise.
3. **The hint on the last Motorbike.** It still says Self-destruct, and on the last Motorbike that forfeits the Round. A different text there is a small follow-up if wanted.
4. **Starting values to tune by playing:** the spot turn rates (half of each turn rate), the standing Fuel rates (a quarter of each moving rate) and the tanks' collider (the band's radius, the tank's height).
5. The Windows zip is over the 30 MiB chat limit: hand it over by path.
