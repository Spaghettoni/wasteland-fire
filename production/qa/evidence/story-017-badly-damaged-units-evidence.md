## Measured facts the code comments point at

- **Where the smoke attaches:** a child of the Unit's Model, at the top centre of its hull (`MeshUnitModel`'s box of the file's vertices, the Gyrocopter's up to 1.4 m and under its rotor): 1.24 m up on a Motorbike, 2.00 on a Buggy (the pennant), 2.70 on a Truck (the antenna's tip) and 2.17 on a hovering Gyrocopter. For the three ground types the point is the top of the world box of their meshes to within 0.02 m (`smoke_begins_at_the_hit_from_the_drawn_body`).
- **Draws in both views and costs one call a view** (`measurements/windowed-fps-run1.txt`, `-run2.txt`): see AC-4. It simulates deterministically in a headless run (a tick counter, no random number), so the checks read exact puff states and two runs are byte-identical.
- **A world-space trail near a view's edge:** not measured on its own. A puff's mesh is a unit quad with a `custom_aabb` of a cube, so that the flat box of a billboard is never tested edge-on by the culling, and no puff vanished at an edge in the recorded frames (the trails of frames 03 and 04 run down the view from the Unit).
- **Under the Round-over pause the smoke stands frozen with the Units:** `limp_home_then_r_ends_the_state` (the pool's tick counter and the 15 puffs of each Unit unchanged over 30 paused ticks); R ends it in its own frame.
- **A puff moved after it entered the tree must reset its interpolation** (the windowed probe above: 28.08 m of sliding without the reset); a puff placed in the tick it was born in and reset is drawn in place on its first frame, and one that is not `top_level` drifts 0.183 m with its moving parent.
- **The first frame of a Round's first state costs 15.6 and 16.4 ms** (the longest of the first 30 frames after the state began, windows 2 of the two `badly_damaged_fps` runs): the smoke scene is instanced and its pool made on that tick. Under the 16.6 ms bar by 0.2 to 1.0 ms and the one frame that comes close. A warm-up before the Round (as `_warm_up_models()` and `_warm_up_explosion()` do) was not made: an instance added in the Round's start would put a smoke node into every older scenario, which the story forbids (R18). Observed, not pinned.
- **A model's world box includes its smoke unless it is left out:** `Quick.model_box()` and `model_bottom()` read every `MeshInstance3D` under a Model, and 15 hidden puffs at the world origin made a Gyrocopter's lowest point -1.00 m; they now skip a mesh whose parent is a `BadlyDamagedSmoke` (`quick_fix_kit.gd`'s `body_meshes()`), which changes no older output (no older scenario has one).
- **A Mine at the Truck's trigger radius:** a Motorbike put 1.5 m from a Mine's centre did not set it off (its box edge touches the 0.8 m trigger exactly), a Truck and a Buggy did; the Mine routes put the Unit on the Mine's centre.

## Consequences recorded for the author (no rule change; AC-2, AC-6)

- **Turning:** at the halved top speed the speed-scaled turn comes to each type's spot turn rate (1.400, 1.150, 0.450, 0.770 rad/s measured), so a badly damaged Unit turns at its spot rate at any speed and each turn takes about twice as long, the circle it drives staying about what it was.

## Deviations from the story and notes

None blocking. Advisory, each judged and listed here:

1. **The smoke's puff is a soft billboard quad, not the explosion's grey ball.** The story says to reuse story 016's smoke piece where it has one. The first build did (a sphere in an unshaded alpha grey, the explosion's technique, its material and its colour family); the recording showed a moving Unit's trail as a row of hard-edged discs and a column as stacked coins (`measurements/look-choice-*.png`).
