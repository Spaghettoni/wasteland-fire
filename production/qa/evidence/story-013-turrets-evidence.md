## Engine-specialist review

One read-only review by the engine specialist (`godot-gdscript-specialist`, a closed list of the story's `src/` files and scenes, a budget of 15 turns; it edited nothing and ran no Godot, only a Python port of `intercept_time`; 24 tool uses, 18 minutes): **APPROVED WITH FINDINGS — no blocker, no major, 2 minor, 5 nit.** Every finding was taken:

1. [minor] `Turret._physics_process` tested `_target == null` but not `is_instance_valid(_target)` (`weapon.gd` does). Taken: the test is now `is_instance_valid(_target)`. A freed Unit compares equal to null in Godot 4, so the old test refused it too (the seeded defect `freed_target_not_guarded` is an equivalent mutant); the change states the intent and `refusals` keeps the behaviour: a target freed under an armed Turret logs nothing and leaves it idle.
2. [minor] `Turret.arm()` took the rules unchecked: rules with no damage matrix would make every Shot push "damage matrix is not assigned", one error per Turret per 0.8 s. Taken: `arm()` refuses such rules with one error naming the Turret and stops its physics processing (`refusals`, seeded defect `arm_accepts_rules_without_a_matrix` killed; that one error is the scenario's one expected engine error).
3. [nit] `restore()` called `super.restore()` first, so a listener of `restored` found a half-restored Turret (the head still turned, the state stale). Taken: the head, the cadence, the state and the aim are reset first, `super.restore()` last (the rotation still before `reset_physics_interpolation()`).
4. [nit] A Turret destroyed while aiming kept `aim_point` and `aim_error`, which the docs say read INF without an aim. Taken: a `destroy()` override forgets them (`fall` reads INF after a fall mid-turn; seeded defect `destroyed_turret_keeps_its_aim` killed).
5. [nit] Two doc lines of `is_flag_locked_for()` in `match_controller.gd` were 102 columns. Taken: re-wrapped (the function is still the file's last, so no earlier line moved).
6. [nit] Section order in `turret.gd` and `player_notice.gd` (the project's order: built-ins, public, private). Taken: reordered.
7. [nit] The class doc's "the table complete (a pair it does not list cannot happen)" was not exact: one tick can take a Turret from IDLE through AIMING to FIRING and back, and a `fired` listener reads FIRING. Taken: one paragraph of the class doc says so.

Not verified by the reviewer, who ran no Godot: that the sight ray reports a concave cliff face from outside under Jolt (the `sight` check's canyon case proves it: a Unit at the canyon Fuel Can is not aimed at while its Shots pass through the rock and hit the Turret), the frame rate and draw calls (AC-8), and the Gyrocopter's rise query against the Turret's `Look` node, which has no direct mesh child (a Gyrocopter driven into a Turret stopped flush at 2.201 m and nothing was logged: `solid` and `no_engine_noise`).

Checked and found sound by the reviewer: the ray queries (excluded RIDs, `hit_from_inside`, masks 83 and 32, the path ray cast only on a tick that can fire), the tick order and `LAUNCH_LAG_TICKS = 0` (derived independently from the order of World's children and the Shot's first move, as measured), `get_real_velocity()`, `intercept_time` (swept against brute force on 600 random cases, no mismatch, every degenerate case right), the barrel's turn and `_error_at`, the read-only properties, `destroy()` and `restore()` timing and legality, the scenes' exports and paths, the lock gate and `is_flag_locked_for()`, and the style rules (static typing, `##` docs, no tuning value in code, no integer literal but 0 and 1 in the `tokens_data` files, no branch naming a Unit type).
