## Measured facts the story asked for

1. **A box zone's latency on 4.7.2 with Jolt** (Measure first 1), `zone_latency_is_measured`: a Buggy driven through at 19.6 m/s is listed **2 ticks after its box first overlaps the zone's and still listed 2 ticks after its box has left it**; a Truck spawned in the zone is listed on the **2nd row** and, by the two-tick rule, given Fuel on the **3rd** (the spawned case matches the Fuel Can's of story 006: listed two ticks after the spawn, giving on the third; a row is the state at the end of a tick, so the driven-in and leaving figures are counted in rows). A Unit that sets off is last given Fuel on its first moving tick (the zone reads its speed before the Unit's own tick).
2. **Real velocities** (Measure first 2): a Unit standing still has a real horizontal speed of **exactly 0.000000000** for all four types, the Gyrocopter hovering included (`stopped_speed_is_above_the_noise`, 110 ticks each); a Truck held against a tank with the throttle on reads 0.0000 with its drive speed at 10.90 m/s; the Buggy parked in the zone and the Truck driven into it with the throttle held both read 0.0000, and the parked Buggy is not moved (0.000 m): **it refuels** (the outcome the story left open). `stopped_speed` 0.1 m/s is therefore far above the noise; the first moving tick of a Truck is computed to read 0.15 m/s (its 9 m/s^2 over one tick), over the threshold. The two crawls on either side of it (0.05 and 0.11 m/s) are refuelled and not.
3. **The fill times per type** (Measure first 3): from empty, 245 ticks for the Motorbike (4.083 s), 247 for the Buggy (4.117 s), 248 for the Truck (4.133 s) and 241 for the Gyrocopter from 5% of a tank (4.017 s), the rule's number to the tick; the story computed about 4.1 to 4.2 s with the standing burn. For a Unit held against a tank with the throttle on its moving burn (2 to 3 Fuel a second) applies instead: the oracle judged every row of that run against the moving rate, and the from-empty time for it (about 4.5 s) is computed, not measured.
4. **The Gyrocopter is listed at its flying height** (Measure first 4): yes. Its body stays on the floor (story 020), so the zone lists it with its model 2.50 m up (`gyrocopter_from_its_base_to_the_zone_p1`/`_p2`, `hovering_gyrocopter_never_runs_dry`, frame 05); the zone's 6 m height is a guard behind that, and the mutant that makes it 1 m is equivalent.
5. **The clearances** (Measure first 5): 1.672 m from the zone's nearest corner to the nearer north tank's wall, 7.736 m to the south tank's, 13.661 m to the far north tank's, 3.000 m to the nearest Wall Panel (`FieldWalls/FieldWallWest/Panel1`), the same at the east zone (`zone_clearances`). The Gyrocopter's straight lane from its Base's spawn point to the zone holds no Tall piece for a body of its half width (0.8 m), and the flight along it ended in a hover with no crash; the ground route is another matter (see below).

## Found while building

- **The centre line from a Gate to the depot is blocked for a ground Unit.** A container 12 x 2.5 m stands on it at x = -58 and x = 58 (`map_01_cover.tscn`, layer 64, so it spans x -64 to -52 and 52 to 64): a Unit driven straight from a Gate to a zone along z = 0 stops against its outer face (a Truck at x = -66.2, its nose on the face at -64), 52 m short of the zone, and stays pressed there (the first try, 2,400 ticks). The ground-reach checks steer north of it (a waypoint at x = +/-46 on z = -3.5, between the container and the wreck at (-53, -8)); a Player would do the same, and the Gyrocopter flies over it. Nothing in the story or the design says the route should be straighter.
- **A Unit spawned where another stands lands on its roof.** `zone_never_runs_dry` stood Player 2's new Truck at the west zone's middle while Player 1's was still there: it came down 1.4 to 1.6 m up, and only a seeded defect (a 1 m tall box, which should have been equivalent) noticed that it was no longer in the zone. Fixed by parking the other Player first; every other module was checked for the same staging.
- **`put()` settles three ticks, so the zone's first gift fell before a recording that started after it.** `put_recorded()` starts the record in the very frame of the spawn, so the whole fill is on the tape (first gift in row 3, from a spot outside the zone: the same Unit put where another stood a moment ago is listed a tick earlier).
- **The stale listing arises**: a Truck Self-destructed in the zone and respawned at its Garage is listed by the zone once, on its Base, for exactly one row (`stale_listed_rows_1`), and the two-tick rule keeps it out.
- **The printed lines that moved**: the runner's `advance_ticks` frame (1485 -> 1541) in 8 older outputs, pinned as exact rewrites from the source. The new scenario's own output prints the zone's `_ready` line (`supply_zone.gd:116`, three expected errors) and the runner's `advance_ticks` line; story 024, which edits `supply_zone.gd`, will have to pin the first for `fuel_building`.
- **TD-006 and a new TD-015** (`docs/tech-debt-register.md`): the scenario set grew by 16 scripts (2,396 lines, each under 250, the kit 246) and the runner by 56 lines (now 1,696); the FuelCan class, its scene and its settings, and the frozen Can scene, are evidence-only code kept for the older scenarios (R19) and logged so nobody deletes them as dead code.

## The frame rate (`fuel_building_fps`, AC-6)

Four windows of 6 s on the real renderer (the Fuel zones drawn, both Players, the first second and every second a window was being staged left out of the steady figures): (1) both Trucks south of the depot, the view from above, (2) each Truck in its zone, the chase view, (3) both Gyrocopters hovering over their zones, (4) a Motorbike and a Buggy side by side in the west zone, from above. **Findings.** The rate is at the display's 120 Hz in almost every second and never under 117 (the budget: 60); the draw calls are 271 for both views together against 1,000, **26 fewer than with the nine Cans** (the likely reason: the two zones' slabs share one mesh and their 24 dashes another, while the nine Cans are nine instances of three meshes each; the measurement is the 271 against 297, the reason is not isolated); story 012's 222 and 223 were a different scene. **The budget's 16.6 ms line is not met on every run**: a single frame over 16.6 ms appears in a steady second in 2 of the 4 runs with the zones (18.63 and 17.21 ms; the other two read 12.87 and 15.65) and in all 3 without them (18.18 to 18.35 ms), in windows that stage nothing. The stripped runs are the same or worse, so the zones are not the cause (the Fuel building does not add a frame cost; it removes some); the likeliest causes are the machine and the older costs TD-013 describes (a frame with a physics tick and the work of both views), and an earlier batch of six runs the same afternoon (not retained: its files were overwritten by this one) read the same way (zones 12.4 to 17.3 ms, no zones 12.9 to 18.8 ms). It is recorded, not fixed here, and is the author's to weigh against the provisional budget.
