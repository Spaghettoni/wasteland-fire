## Measured facts the story asked for

All on 4.7.2 with Jolt, `measurements/probe21-output-*.txt`, before the first source edit (a grounded Gyrocopter, mask 1, read after its own tick by a node at physics priority 100), and confirmed by the scenario's checks:

- **The Floor:** no slide collision at all on any tick of a flight over the Floor at any speed (0 of 120 ticks up to 16.4 m/s); the Floor (normal straight up) on every tick the Gyrocopter stands or turns on the spot. It never counts: the floor test is the normal against the up direction and the body's floor angle. (The engine review's reading of why the Floor shows only while the Gyrocopter is not moving: Jolt drops contacts whose normal does not point against the motion; the probe measured the fact, not that cause.)
- **Head-on at top speed** into a corner tower's flat face, a water tower leg's face, a depot tank and a Turret (each cylinder on a line through its axis): on the first contact tick the collider is the piece, the normal its face's (or the radial one), `is_on_wall()` true and the previous tick's real velocity 16.4 m/s, so the arrival is 16.4 m/s; on that tick the real velocity is cut to 8.52 m/s by the contact, and the next tick's arrival reads that, then zero. The Gyrocopter stops dead (the 15 degree stop angle applies in GROUNDED mode). So a test on this tick's own velocity would read a head-on hit as half the speed or less.
- **Pinned** with the throttle held for 3 s: a contact on every tick after the first, an arrival of 0 on every one, the command at 16.4 m/s. A test on the command would destroy it a quarter of a second after it crept into the leg.
- **A turn on the spot** flush against each Tall kind (from rest): contacts on 39 to 48 ticks of 480, the body pushed out at up to 0.58 m/s (0.563 to 0.577 over the four kinds), an arrival of 0 throughout (the box's corner at the 0.77 rad/s spot turn moves at about 1.1 m/s, computed; only part of that goes into the face).
- **The run-up:** from a standstill the speed climbs 0.25 m/s a tick (15 m/s² over 1/60 s), so 4.0 m/s comes on the 16th tick, 0.57 m from rest (computed: 0.53 m); more than about half a metre of full throttle straight at a Tall piece crashes the Gyrocopter and only a tap, a turn or a graze stops it.
- **The angle edge:** along a tower's face at the top speed the first-contact arrival is 16.4 x sin(angle to the face) to the third decimal (5 degrees 1.429, 13 degrees 3.689, 14.0 3.968, 14.1 3.995, 14.2 4.023, 15 4.245, 20 5.609); the threshold is asin(4 / 16.4) = 14.12 degrees (computed 14.1). A graze slides on along the face at 16.4 x cos(angle); the tick after the first contact can read the part of the contact tick's motion that went into the face, never more than the first arrival (5 degrees: 1.429, then 0.994, then 0).
- **The grazing offsets** (a line parallel to the axis, the front corner meeting the cylinder's side), measured: the Turret (radius 1.0 m) arrives at 5.03 m/s 1.75 m off the axis, 4.49 at 1.76, 3.90 at 1.77, 3.19 at 1.78 and misses at 1.80; a depot tank (2.56 m) 4.93 at 3.24, 4.50 at 3.26, 4.04 at 3.28, 3.51 at 3.30 and misses at 3.38. The 4.0 m/s crossing is at about 1.768 m and 3.281 m, against 1.770 m and 3.283 m computed (0.8 m plus 0.97 of the radius): the story's formula holds to 2 mm. A water tower leg (0.6 m square) is met face on up to 1.05 m off its axis (arrival 16.0 m/s) and missed from 1.10 m.
- **The spawns:** the engine's own `get_real_velocity()` keeps the last life's value across `destroy()` and `spawn()` (-10 m/s read right after a spawn flush against a leg, for a life that flew at it at 10 m/s), which is why the Unit keeps its own copy of the arrival velocity and `reset_motion()` zeroes it. A Gyrocopter put down flush against a leg with the throttle held reads an arrival of 0 on every tick; 0.05 m from it, 1.00 m/s at the touch; 0.5 m from it, 3.74 m/s (under 4.0 by the run-up of a spawn). Flown straight out of each of the four Garage spots at full throttle it touches nothing.
- **The sea edge:** head-on at top speed into each of the four shores the first contact names a shore body (`ShoreNW7`, `ShoreNW4`, `ShoreSW4`, `ShoreNE7`) at an arrival of 16.4 m/s: only the group tells it apart from a Tall piece.
- **Reversing:** the reverse speed is 6.0 m/s and the rule reads the speed along the face whichever way the Unit travels: backing tail first into the corner tower at the full reverse key, the Gyrocopter arrives at 6.00 m/s and is destroyed on the tick its control touches the face (`reverse_crash`). Question 6.
