## The frame rate

AC-5's budget (`.claude/docs/technical-preferences.md`: the lowest one-second rate 60 fps or more, a frame of at most 16.6 ms, at most 1,000 draw calls, both views together), on the 120 Hz display. `ammo_buildings_fps` runs four windows of 6 s with both buildings and all four zones drawn (both Trucks short of the zones, the view from above; each Truck in its ammunition zone, the chase view; both Gyrocopters hovering over their zones; the Fuel zone and an ammunition zone, the view from above), every Unit put down with an empty gun so the zones work. Three runs (`measurements/fps/ammo-1.txt` to `ammo-3.txt`): the lowest steady second **120 fps** each time (the first second and every second a window was being staged are left out, as in the earlier fps scenarios; the raw lowest second is 66 to 71), the longest steady frame **12.70, 14.19 and 12.79 ms**, **276 draw calls** at most for both views (the largest window, the chase view in the zones), 256,988 primitives. So the rate, the draw calls and the frame line hold in all three runs.

The A/B on the same build (`fuel_building_fps` windows, the depot's zones and the Gyrocopters, with the buildings left on by a scratch copy that declares `AMMO_BUILDINGS`, and with the runner's strip as shipped): **287 draw calls with the buildings and 271 without** (+16 for the two buildings, 14 meshes each, in the frames that look at them; 405,444 against 404,844 primitives), and the lowest steady second 119 to 120 fps in both. Finding: a steady-second frame **over 16.6 ms appeared in 2 of 7 runs with the buildings** (18.72 and 17.57 ms, both at t = 28.2 s in window 4, where Player 1's Motorbike and Player 2's Buggy stand in the west Fuel zone and neither building is in either view; the other five runs peaked at 12.55 to 15.40 ms) **and in none of 7 without** (12.75 to 15.12 ms). Story 023 recorded the same kind of single long frame in a steady second with and without its zones, and its `/story-done` logged it as TD-016 (a frame of 17 to 19 ms with the zones and 18.2 to 18.4 ms with them stripped, in windows that stage nothing: the machine or an older cost). Seven pairs cannot say whether the buildings (16 more shadow-casting meshes in the shadow pass, which reaches 100 m from the camera) cost an occasional frame; `ammo_buildings_fps` itself, which looks at the buildings, had none in three runs. It is for the author to weigh, with TD-016.
