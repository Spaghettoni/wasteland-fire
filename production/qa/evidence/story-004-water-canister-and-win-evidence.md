## Notes, gaps and decisions recorded here

- **Settle rule (SPAWN_SETTLE_TICKS = 3).** Added after the preflight measured the ghost overlap above: a freshly spawned Player's pick-ups and deliveries wait three physics frames. It is a documented engine latency, not a `MatchRules` value. The stamp is pushed on across a tree pause like the respawn waits (restored by hand after a fix pass removed it as unnamed by the design; decision log 2026-10-01).
- **Rules the design left open, decided here:** two deliveries on one tick go to the lower Player index, like a pick-up tie; an enemy canister lying inside the Player's own Base zone is picked up and delivered on the same tick (touching it wins); `round_over` keeps its name as the one exception to the past-tense signal rule; named constants (`NO_WINNER`, `CanisterRules.NONE`, `RoundRestartInput.ACTION_RESTART`), the `CanisterRules` helper's small API, the Cargo inspector group and `drop_at(at)` (not `position`, which would shadow `Node3D.position`) are accepted implementation detail; `Base._ready()` warns when its canister or seat is unassigned.
- **Carry offset:** the design's body-top mount (0, 1.0, 0.6) hid 73 % of the Unit's cream nose from the chase camera; the tail mount (0, 0.5, 1.7) hides none and is the data value.
- **Harness runner stays `INHERIT`:** setting the runner to `ALWAYS` would make the whole game ignore the Round-over pause (its children inherit the mode); coroutines awaiting `physics_frame` and injected input keep working while paused, so tick counting needed no change.
- **Gap:** the rule "a Unit carries at most one canister" has no retained scenario step; the lens probe above measured it, and a seeded defect that lets a Unit carry two survives the retained set (TD-006). The 1920×1080 reading of the HUD was taken headless from the window geometry only; no frame at that size was rendered.
- **Recording caveats (TD-009):** a windowed showcase recording made while the Godot window loses focus can lose a held key (the engine releases pressed keys on focus-out while the runner's cache believes them held): one of the lens's three recordings failed its premise check that way and was discarded. A recording made while the display is asleep or the window occluded repeats frames while the simulation runs on (the first recording of this close-out, 269 duplicated frames); the retained recording was made after waking the display and the engine reported 2726 frames for 2726 files.
