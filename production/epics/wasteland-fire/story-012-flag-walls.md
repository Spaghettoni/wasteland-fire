**Requirement**: The developer's request of 2026-10-07, verbatim: "I want new features in this game. Turrets (towers), mines, walls around flag. Turrets are per team, each team has its own turrets, turrets attack opponents vehicles, cant be built or captured - must be destroyed in order to get to flag. Walls are around flag and also have to be destroyed (motorbike cant destroy walls). Truck can land mines, they explode after land vehicles move over them - gyrocopter wont trigger mines." For turrets and mines the request lifts the exclusion of 2026-09-29 ("turrets, mines, drones and AI units are excluded", the former Hazards section); drones and AI units stay excluded. What the card leaves open is built on the readings marked "(reading, 2026-10-07)" in `design/rules.md`; this story's are listed under Questions for the author below.

## Acceptance Criteria

*From the developer's request, the author's Flag Wall card and the answers and readings of 2026-10-07, scoped to this story, and the developer's answers to its questions of 2026-10-08 (AC-9):*

- [x] AC-1: Each Base on Map 01 has four Flag Walls, one in each gap between its water tower's four legs, closing a box around its Flag's seat: about 2 m tall (a little taller than every Unit's collider, the Truck's 1.6 m being the tallest), 1 m thick, their outer faces flush with the legs' (the box spans Base-local x -2.3 to 2.3 and z -9.3 to -4.7; world x -115.3 to -110.7 at Base A and 110.7 to 115.3 at Base B, z -2.3 to 2.3), mirror images across x = 0, dark like the Base walls. While all four Flag Walls of a Base stand, nobody takes its Flag from any side: the other Player's Motorbike driven at the box from each of its four sides, at both Bases (W for Player 1, Up for Player 2), is stopped at a Flag Wall and the Flag's touch never reports it, so the Flag stays on its seat (the owner's HUD keeps "Your Flag is at home" and the raider's never shows "You carry the enemy Flag"). A Gyrocopter may fly over a Flag Wall into the box, but only a Motorbike takes a Flag and the Flag's touch never lists a Gyrocopter. In the view from above, while a Unit faces a closed box from any of its four sides within 20 m of the seat, the top of the seated Flag shows over the near Flag Wall.
- [x] AC-2: A Flag Wall stops what the Map's cover stops: a Motorbike, a Buggy or a Truck driven head-on into one at full throttle stops flush at its face as at a wall, and a Shot of any type (Space for Player 1, Period for Player 2) whose path meets a Flag Wall ends on it, on its face, or at the muzzle when the shooter stands pressed against it and fires from inside it, as at the cover. The Gyrocopter flies over a Flag Wall at full speed without slowing, its model rising over it and settling back as over the cover, so it can enter the box and leave over another Flag Wall; the legs still stop it. Nothing that watches Units sees a Flag Wall: the Base's zone, the Flag's touch and the test for a free spawn spot ignore it.
- [x] AC-3: A Flag Wall has 40 hit points. A Shot that ends on the other Player's Flag Wall takes the shooter's damage times the damage matrix's multiplier off it: x1.0 for the Buggy (12 a Shot), the Truck (25) and the Gyrocopter (15), so a Flag Wall falls to the 4th Buggy Shot, the 2nd Truck Shot or the 3rd Gyrocopter Shot, and x0.0 for the Motorbike, whose Shot still ends on the Flag Wall and takes nothing, however long it fires. A Player's own Shots, of every type, end on that Player's own Flag Walls and take nothing: no Player damages their own. Damage between Units is unchanged.
- [x] AC-4: A Flag Wall whose hit points reach 0 falls apart on that tick and shows as low rubble from then on. From the next tick at the latest it blocks nothing (which of the two ticks is measured first: Implementation Notes, Measure first): a Unit drives over the rubble and a Shot flies over it as over open ground, and the gap it leaves (3.4 m between the legs, 2.6 m between the neighbouring Flag Walls) lets the 1.4 m Motorbike in. Any one fallen Flag Wall opens the box: with each single Flag Wall down in turn, the four sides at both Bases, the other Player's Motorbike drives in, is handed the Flag ("You carry the enemy Flag" in its HUD) and drives or reverses out of the box. Once at each Base, after a Truck has broken the Gate-side Flag Wall (Player 1's Space at Base B, Player 2's Period at Base A), that Player's Motorbike takes the Flag through the gap and, brought into its own Base, delivers it ("Player 1 wins!" after Player 1's raid on Base B, "Player 2 wins!" after Player 2's on Base A).
- [x] AC-5: A fallen Flag Wall stays down for the rest of the Round: no key or rule builds, repairs or captures one, and no destruction, respawn, Swap (Tab or Enter inside the own Base) or Flag drop brings it back. When the Round is over ("Player 1 wins!", "Player 2 wins!" or "Nobody wins!"), R ("Press R to play again") brings back every Flag Wall of both Bases on the restart, with 40 hit points, solid, the rubble gone, so the next Round starts with both boxes closed; every Flag is on its seat and both Units are benched in their Garages before the Flag Walls come back, so a Flag Wall never closes on a Unit or on a dropped Flag.
- [x] AC-6: New Units appear on the Garage's two side spots, 4 m either side of its middle, and never on the middle spot, whose straight lane to the Gate the box now closes. Each Base has a first spot and a spare, and the two Bases' first spots are mirror images across x = 0 (Base A's at world (-125, 0, -4), Base B's at (125, 0, -4)); the first spot is used at the start of a Round, after a destruction, after a Swap and after R, and the spare whenever the other Player's Unit stands on the first. From either spot of either Base every type (Motorbike, Buggy, Truck, Gyrocopter) drives straight out through the Gate on the throttle alone (W for Player 1, Up for Player 2), touching no Flag Wall, leg, Gate post or wall: the Truck, the widest, passes 0.3 m from a Gate post and 0.5 m from the box.
- [x] AC-7: A delivery and an owner's return work as before. A Carrier with the other Player's Flag wins the moment its own Base reports it inside, whatever stands of its own box. An owner who picks up its own dropped Flag and brings it into its own Base has it seated on its seat inside the box ("Your Flag is at home" again). A Flag dropped inside a breached box (its Carrier destroyed there, by a Shot or a Self-destruct) is taken through the breach by its owner's Motorbike and seated on that same tick; a Flag dropped against the outside of a standing Flag Wall is taken from that side, and its touch never reaches through the Flag Wall.
- [x] AC-8: Every value is data: a Flag Wall's hit points and type id in its stats `.tres`, the Motorbike's x0.0 against it as one row of `damage_matrix.tres` (every other pair keeps the data's x1.0 default), its size, physics layer and look in its scene, the four places in `compound_base.tscn` and each Base's two spawn spots in `compound_base.tscn` and `map_01.tscn`; no number and no Unit type is written in code, and nothing new is shown as text. The evidence scenarios written before this story run without the Flag Walls and with the Story 007 spawn spots, and keep their outputs: they stay byte-identical, apart from exact rewrites of the line numbers that intended engine backtraces print.
- [x] AC-9: A standing Flag Wall shows how much of its hit points it has lost (the developer's answer to question 1, 2026-10-08): it looks whole while it keeps 80% of them or more, shows its first damaged look below 80% (darker than the whole wall and cracked, with a few chunks at its foot) and its second below 35% (darker still and more cracked, with more chunks), and falls into the rubble of AC-4 at 0. So every type but the Motorbike changes the look of the other Player's Flag Wall with its first Shot: a Truck's first Shot leaves 15 of 40 hit points, the first damaged look, and its second drops the wall; a Buggy's Shots leave the first look after Shots 1 and 2 and the second after Shot 3; a Gyrocopter's the first look, then the second; however long a Motorbike fires, the look does not change. Exactly one look shows at a time, and a Flag Wall at exactly 80% or 35% of its hit points still shows the look above it. Only the look changes: the collider, the physics layer, the height and what stops at a Flag Wall stay those of the whole wall until it falls, and the chunks have no collider. Nothing repairs a hurt Flag Wall before R: it keeps its look through a destruction, a respawn and a Swap, and R brings back the whole look with the hit points (AC-5). The two fractions are data in `flag_wall_stats.tres` and the looks are nodes and materials of the Flag Wall's scene; a scene whose looks do not match the fractions logs one error and shows only the whole look until the wall falls.

## Implementation Notes

*At `minimal` there is no ADR; these are pointers, not decisions:*

- **Structure** (new, `src/gameplay/defences/structure.gd`, `class_name Structure extends StaticBody3D`): the damageable static piece a Flag Wall is now and a Turret will be (story 013). Exports `stats: StructureStats` (required), `intact: Node3D` (shown while it stands and no damaged look is due: Damaged looks, below) and `wreck: Node3D` (shown once it has fallen; optional); a plain `player_index: int = -1`, set once by SplitScreen; read-only `type_id` (`stats.type_id`, guarded as the Unit's is at `unit.gd:291`: empty while the stats are refused), `hit_points` and `is_standing`. `apply_damage(amount)` ignores a fallen piece and an amount that is not above zero, NaN included (the guard of `unit.gd:452`), subtracts and clamps at zero, calls `destroy()` at zero and otherwise emits `hit_points_changed`. `destroy()` hides `intact` (or the damaged look it showed), shows `wreck` and zeroes `collision_layer` and `collision_mask`, the way a Unit leaves play (`unit.gd:484-497`; never `PROCESS_MODE_DISABLED` inside a tick, `unit.gd:139-143`), then emits `hit_points_changed(0, max)` and `destroyed`. `restore()` refills the hit points, puts back the layer and mask the scene had at `_ready()`, shows `intact`, hides `wreck` and the damaged looks and emits `hit_points_changed` and `restored`; call it only while no Unit overlaps the piece (a body that regains its layer over a Unit throws it out, as a Unit put down inside another is: `unit.gd:150-158`), which a restart guarantees. Missing or unusable stats (`StructureStats.first_problem()`) are one error in `_ready()`. The piece then stays as plain, solid geometry and logs nothing more: `type_id` returns `&""`, `is_standing` is false, and `apply_damage()` and `restore()` do nothing. The guard matters because the Shot reads `type_id` from every body it may damage (The Shot's contract, below), so a bare `stats.type_id` would raise a script error on every Shot that hits a piece with no stats. No `_physics_process`. The signals are for display and the evidence; no game code listens to them in this story, and the piece knows no Unit, Base or Round.
- **StructureStats** (`src/gameplay/defences/structure_stats.gd`, `class_name StructureStats extends Resource`): `type_id`, `display_name`, `max_hit_points` and, for AC-9, `damaged_below`, every default zero or empty (the rule of `unit_stats.gd:52-56`: a tuned value equal to the script's default would vanish from the `.tres`), and `first_problem()` after `unit_stats.gd:285-297` (a type id, hit points above zero; for AC-9 also the `damaged_below` fractions; a NaN is refused in both, the tests being written as "not above").
- **Damaged looks** (AC-9, added 2026-10-08 at the developer's answer to question 1): `StructureStats.damaged_below`, an `Array[float]` of fractions of `max_hit_points`, highest first, empty by default (a Structure that leaves it out keeps the intact look until it falls), checked by `first_problem()` (each above 0, below 1 and below the one before it). It is an `Array[float]`, not a `PackedFloat32Array`: 0.8 as a 32-bit float is 0.80000001, which would put a wall at exactly 32 of 40 hit points below it. `Structure.damaged_looks`, an `Array[Node3D]` export (`node_paths`), holds one node per fraction: `_show_look()` shows exactly one of `intact`, the damaged look of the piece's stage (how many fractions its hit points are strictly below) and `wreck`, on every hit the piece survives (before `hit_points_changed`), at the fall and at `restore()`. A list that does not match the fractions (another count, an unassigned entry) is one error in `_ready()`, and the piece then shows only its intact look until it falls. The Flag Wall's looks are `MeshDamaged1` and `MeshDamaged2`: direct MeshInstance3D children that share `Mesh`'s BoxMesh, so the Gyrocopter's rise (`kit_unit_model.gd` `_drawn_top()`, which reads every direct GeometryInstance3D child, shown or hidden) and every face measurement stay those of the whole wall. Each has its own `material_override` under `src/gameplay/defences/materials/` (a grey `albedo_color` below `wall.tres`'s 0.55, the second below the first, with a crack pattern) and chunk children at the panel's foot on both faces, outside its footprint, with no collider and no shadow: four chips in the first look, and in the second the same four and four larger ones, so the pile grows toward the rubble. Data: `damaged_below = Array[float]([0.8, 0.35])` in `flag_wall_stats.tres`.

It offers `apply_damage()`; it has a `type_id` that is a StringName (`DamageMatrix.multiplier()` takes typed StringName arguments, `damage_matrix.gd:41`, so a body without one would be a script error at the hit); and it is not the shooter's Player's (its `player_index`, read with `get()` and taken as -1 when it has none, differs from the shooter's, or one of the two is below 0). Only then `apply_damage(damage * matrix.multiplier(shooter type, its type_id))`. Every collider on the mask still ends the Shot (`queue_free()`), so a refused target is a stop with no damage. A Unit already meets the contract (`unit.gd:289-293`, `:451-458`).
