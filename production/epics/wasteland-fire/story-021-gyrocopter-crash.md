## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 6, the developer's answers and the reading of 2026-10-10, scoped to this story:*

- [x] AC-1: A Gyrocopter flown straight into a Tall piece at full throttle from a run-up is destroyed on the tick it meets it: at each Tall kind, a corner tower's flat face, a water-tower leg's face, a depot Fuel tank and a Turret (each cylinder on a line through its axis), for both Players at both Bases, and at the depot from outside it and from its middle, as `tanks_stop` runs. The crash comes on the tick of the first contact whose arrival speed along the contact's normal reaches the crash speed (4.0 m/s), measured from the motion the Gyrocopter arrived with, and the rest of that tick does not run.
- [x] AC-2: A crash is a destruction like any other: it takes one Gyrocopter Token from its Player, the respawn wait runs and the Player chooses the next type as after any destruction, and story 016's explosion plays once, at the Gyrocopter's drawn model at the flying height, in both views. The piece takes no damage: a Turret keeps its hit points and its look, and nothing falls, moves or changes. Two crashes on one tick, one per Player, take one Token each.
- [x] AC-3: Below the crash speed a Tall piece only stops the Gyrocopter, as today: a graze at top speed along a tower's face shallower than the crash angle (computed: about 14 degrees to the face), a slow nudge into each Tall kind at 3.5 m/s, a turn on the spot flush against each Tall kind, and a Gyrocopter pinned against a leg with the throttle held for 3 s (its commanded speed reaches top speed while its real motion stays zero) all leave it alive, stopped or sliding as today. The edges are measured on both sides: 0.1 m/s either side of the crash speed head-on at a tower's face, a degree either side of the computed angle at top speed, and the grazing offset at a Turret and at a tank.
- [x] AC-4: Nothing else crashes it: the Floor (on every tick of every flight), the sea edge (each of the four shores flown into head-on at top speed: stopped and alive, as today), every low piece (flown over with no contact), a ground Unit and another Gyrocopter (passed through). No ground type ever crashes, its crash speed being 0: a Motorbike, a Buggy and a Truck driven at full throttle into a corner tower, a leg, a tank and a Turret stop as before.
- [x] AC-5: A spawn is never an arrival: a Gyrocopter appearing in its Garage at the Round's first spawn, at a respawn after a crash and after a Swap at the own Base never crashes on its spawn tick or after it from the jump of its spawn, also when a tool puts it down flush against a leg, and it carries none of its last life's motion. Flown straight out of each Garage spot of both Bases at full throttle, it leaves the Base alive.
- [x] AC-6: The crash lands in the tick it happens, in the Gyrocopter's own tick after its move and before that frame's loss check. On the tick a Player's last Motorbike is destroyed, the other Player's Gyrocopter crashing on the same tick costs its Token on that tick and changes neither the winner nor, on a double loss's tick, "Nobody wins!". A crash and a hit on the same tick (a Unit's Shot, a Turret's Shot) destroy it once and take one Token, and a tank that empties on the tick it would crash destroys it once, by the Fuel. Once the Round is over nothing crashes: a Gyrocopter frozen at top speed in front of a leg is still there, alive, until R. A crash never ends a Round by itself: only the destruction of a Player's last Motorbike does.
- [x] AC-7: Every value is data: the crash speed is a UnitStats value in m/s (0, the default, meaning never), 4.0 in `gyrocopter_stats.tres`, left out of the other three types' files and refused by `UnitStats.first_problem()` when negative; the sea edge's bodies are told apart by a group stored in `map_01_terrain.tscn`, not a layer; no number is a constant in code and no code names a type. Every older retained output stays byte-identical: every scenario that does not declare this story's opt-in `GYRO_CRASH` runs with crash speed 0 on every type, proven by a seeded "runner leaves the crash on" defect (`depot_tanks`'s Gyrocopter would be destroyed at the first tank); the baseline is a capture taken twice at the start of this story, before the first edit: the 54 retained headless outputs of the regress-54 list plus the outputs stories 015 to 020 added, the three that differ from their retained files since 73de824 (`gyro_cover`, `flag_walls` and `flag_walls_showcase`) compared as they print at the start.

- **The test (AC-1, AC-3, AC-4).** In the Unit's tick, right after `move_and_slide()` (`unit.gd:405`) and beside the `_blocked` rule (`:406`), in the shape of the Fuel crash (`:73-82, 672-682`: `destroy()` from the Unit's own tick, after which the rest of the tick does not run). Its arrival speed is the Unit's real velocity of the previous tick, read after that tick's move (`get_real_velocity()`, as `:406` reads it), along the normal into the piece; at or above `crash_speed` the Unit is destroyed. Never the commanded velocity: the speed is a command (`:60-66`), so a Gyrocopter pinned against a leg with the throttle held has a commanded velocity at top speed and a real one at zero, and a test on the command would destroy it about a quarter of a second after it crept into the leg. The stored velocity is zeroed where the motion is (`destroy()` and `spawn()` both reset it: `:500`, `:563-564`), so a new Unit carries none of its last life's motion and the jump of a spawn is never an arrival. What is tall is left to the mask, as everywhere in the game: the Gyrocopter's is the map layer alone (`src/gameplay/units/data/gyrocopter_stats.tres:31`), so its body meets no low piece; no layer is added. `unit.gd` is 767 lines (TD-010): keep the test to about 20 lines, or put the reading of the collisions in a small static helper the tick calls (the build's call).

## Out of Scope

- Damage to the piece rammed, and ramming as an attack: the piece takes no damage.
- A crash of a ground type, into a low piece, into a Unit or between two Gyrocopters.
- A new physics layer for the Tall pieces.
- The explosion's look (story 016), a wreck, sound.
- A crash at the Map's edge.
