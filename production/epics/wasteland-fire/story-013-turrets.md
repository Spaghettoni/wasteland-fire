**Requirement**: The developer's request of 2026-10-07, verbatim: "I want new features in this game. Turrets (towers), mines, walls around flag. Treba ich rozbiť, aby sa dalo k vlajke.* — "Each team has its own; they shoot only at the enemy. Automatic, a turning barrel; the players do not control them; they cannot be built or occupied. ## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's board card Vežičky and the answers of 2026-10-07, scoped to this story:*

- [x] AC-1: Each Base has two Turrets outside its Gate, one on each side of it, a few metres in front of the Gate wall, and the yard stays clear of them: Base-local (−10, 0, −19) and (10, 0, −19), 10 m either side of the Gate's axis with their centres 4 m in front of the Gate wall's outer face, so world (−101, 0, ±10) at Base A and (101, 0, ±10) at Base B, mirror images across x = 0 within 0.1 m. Each carries one part in its Base's Team colour, orange at Base A (Player 1's) and teal at Base B (Player 2's), the rest neutral, and its barrel turns on a head on top, so where it aims shows in the view from above. A Turret is solid: every Unit type, the Gyrocopter included, driven into it at full throttle (W for Player 1, Up for Player 2) stops at its face as at a depot Fuel tank, and every Shot ends on it. Every type still leaves and enters through the Gate without touching either Turret, each run recorded up to its first wall contact with its least gap to either Turret: out of both of story 012's Garage spawn spots of both Bases straight on to Base-local z −30; from each spot with a hard turn (A or D for Player 1, Left or Right for Player 2) held at full throttle from the moment its tail clears the Gate wall's outer face, once toward the spot's own side and once across the Gate toward the far side; and in through the Gate from 20 m out, on the two lanes at Base-local x = ±4 (from z −35) and on the two lines 45 degrees off the Gate's axis that cross the middle of the Gate wall's outer face, Base-local (0, 0, −15) (from 20 m along each). Computed: a turn toward its own side passes the near Turret with 2.99 m (Buggy) to 3.57 m (Truck); a turn across the Gate passes the far Turret with 0.61 m (Buggy) and 0.76 m (Motorbike), while the Truck and the Gyrocopter keep 3.72 m and 4.37 m from both Turrets, and the Motorbike, the Buggy and the Gyrocopter then meet the far corner tower (a wall contact to record, not a defect); 3.8 m on the lanes (Truck); 2.04 m for a Truck coming in at 45 degrees.
- [x] AC-2: A Turret aims and fires only at the other Player's Unit while it is in play: Base A's two at Player 2's Unit, Base B's two at Player 1's, every type, the Gyrocopter included. It never takes its own Player's Unit as its target: with only that Unit in reach and in sight, its barrel does not move and nothing is fired, and while that Unit stands between it and the other Player's Unit it holds fire; never at a benched, choosing or destroyed Unit. It fires only at a target its Shot can reach, 35 m from its aimed muzzle (where its Shot leaves once the barrel is on the aim: 1.6 m from the Turret's centre toward the aim point, at the shooting height), the Buggy's range: a Unit parked at least 50 degrees off the barrel's rest heading with its centre 34.5 m from that muzzle is fired on and hit, and one at 35.5 m never is; and it fires only when it sees the target and the Shot's path is clear: whatever stops a Shot hides a target from it, a Base wall or corner tower, the cover (a Gyrocopter over a wreck too: the wreck's face meets the line first), a Flag Wall, the other Turret, a Unit. A cliff hides a target too, although every Shot passes through cliffs (a direct line of sight: told to the developer on 2026-10-07, no objection), so the canyon-side Turret of each Base holds fire at a Unit at its side's canyon Fuel Can, about 21 m away behind the canyon's south wall, while that Unit's Shots pass through the rock and hit it (recorded, not a defect).
- [x] AC-3: Its barrel turns at 90 degrees per second (a 90-degree swing takes 1.0 s) toward the point where its Shot will meet the target, led from the target's own velocity, and it fires only when the barrel is on that point: one Shot every 0.8 s (48 ticks, half the Buggy's rate) for as long as a target is in reach and in sight, with no ammunition to run out, along its barrel at the one shooting height (0.5 m) and 60 m/s, each hit taking 12 × the damage matrix's multiplier (1.0 against every type) off the Unit (a Motorbike's HUD reads "HP 28 / 40" after one). Against a Unit that holds its line and speed every Shot it fires hits, a Motorbike crossing at its top speed 20 m and 30 m away included; a Unit that turns hard while the Shot flies can make it miss, and a Motorbike crossing at top speed close by (inside about 15 m) goes round it faster than the barrel turns, so it holds fire there (both recorded, not defects).
- [x] AC-4: A Turret has 100 hit points, and every Unit type's Shots (Space for Player 1, Period for Player 2) take their damage × 1.0 off it, the Motorbike's included, so it falls to 20 Motorbike Shots, 9 Buggy, 4 Truck or 7 Gyrocopter Shots. A Player's own Shots end on that Player's Turrets and take nothing, and a Turret's Shot that meets its own Player's Unit, Turret or Flag Wall ends there and takes nothing (a Turret's Shot that misses the other Player's Unit just inside its Gate can end on its own Gate-side Flag Wall, which keeps its 40 hit points). At 0 it stops on that tick, no turn and no Shot (Shots already in flight still land), and falls apart into low rubble that stops no Unit and no Shot and stays down for the rest of the Round. Nothing builds, repairs or takes over a Turret: no key acts on one but the fire keys.
- [x] AC-5: The hard rule (decided 2026-10-07): while either Turret of a Base stands, the other Player cannot take that Base's Flag. Player 2's Motorbike that reaches Base A's Flag through a broken Flag Wall and touches it picks nothing up, and Player 2's view shows "Destroy the turrets first" for as long as it touches it, Player 1's view never (the same for Player 1 at Base B); with one Turret down the Flag is still locked; on the first tick both are down the Flag is taken as before, by a Motorbike already touching it too, and the line goes. The owner is never locked: a Player's Motorbike picks up their own dropped Flag wherever it lies, standing Turrets or not, and the line never shows for it; nor for a Motorbike that already carries a Flag (its own, picked up again), which could not take the other Player's anyway (reading, 2026-10-07). The line is one line of text in that Player's own view, clear of the HUD band, the Unit choice panel, the out-of-Fuel hint and the Round-over screen, gone when the Round ends, with nothing clipped in the 640 × 720 view.
- [x] AC-6: A Turret's kill is a destruction like any other (`design/rules.md`, Destruction and respawn: "Every way of being destroyed counts"): it takes one Token of the destroyed type (a destroyed Motorbike turns the HUD's "Motorbike Tokens: 5" into 4), a Carrier it destroys drops the Flag it carried at the wreck, on the ground and in sight, and a Turret that destroys a Player's last Motorbike ends the Round in that physics tick, with "Player 1 wins!" or "Player 2 wins!". A Turret's kill of one Player's last Motorbike in the same tick as the other Player's Self-destruct of their last Motorbike outside their own Base (Tab for Player 1, Enter for Player 2) is the double loss: "Nobody wins!", announced once.
- [x] AC-7: The restart (R, on the Round-over screen) restores every Turret of both Bases, whatever the Round left of it: 100 hit points, standing and solid again with its rubble gone, its barrel back at its rest heading, straight out of its Gate, with no visible swing, its cadence ready and idle until a target comes, so its Base's Flag is locked again. No Shot of the Round before flies on into the next one.
- [x] AC-8: The frame rate holds with the Flag Walls and the Turrets in: windowed on the dev machine at 1280 × 720, with both Players' Trucks in the view from above in front of the other Player's Gate under both of its Turrets' fire, and measured as `map_fps` measures it (one sample a second, the first left out), the lowest one-second frame rate is at least 60 fps and the draw calls of both views together are at most 1,000, the provisional budget (`map_fps` measured 119.7 fps steady and at most 221 and 222 draw calls after story 011; the four Turrets add about 15 and the eight Flag Walls about 4, computed).
- [x] AC-9: Every value and switch is data: the Turret's hit points, damage, cadence, range, Shot speed, barrel turn rate, muzzle, aim tolerance and the layers that hide a target from it, and whether it locks its Base's Flag, in `turret_stats.tres`; its places, its look and its Team-colour part in the Map's scenes; its multipliers in `damage_matrix.tres` (no new row: every pair with the Turret reads the 1.0 default); the notice's text in its scene, through `tr()`. No tuning value is written in code: every gameplay number comes from `turret_stats.tres` or MatchRules (the intercept's own mathematical constants are allowed); the files the `tokens_data` lint reads (`flag_rules.gd`, `match_controller.gd`, `player_notice.gd`) gain no integer literal but 0 and 1; and no branch names a Unit type. The evidence scenarios written before this story, story 012's included, run with the Turrets and the notice line taken out and keep their outputs: byte-identical, or identical after exact rewrites of engine backtrace line numbers and source counts, listed in the evidence.

- **Wiring, no tree search.** `SplitScreen._ready()` (`split_screen.gd:139-151`) is where story 012 hands each Base's Structures their Player index before `begin()`; there it also hands each Turret of `bases[p].structures` the other Player's Unit (`units[1 − p]`: one node per Player for the whole Round, retyped in place: `unit.gd:171-172`), World as the parent of its Shots (where the Weapons put theirs: `weapon.gd:113`) and `match_controller.rules` (`damage_matrix`, `shooting_height`, `shot_collision_mask`: `match_rules.tres:17-19`). - **The tick, a small state machine with its table in the class doc** (`.claude/rules/gameplay-code.md:15`): IDLE → AIMING when the target is in play (`Unit.is_alive`, polled: new code never connects to `Unit.destroyed`, whose link count `tokens_data` prints, `tokens_data_kit.gd:113-115`), in reach and in sight, both measured from the aimed muzzle (Sight and path); AIMING → IDLE when it no longer is; AIMING → FIRING on a tick when the barrel is within `aim_tolerance` of the aim point, the path is clear and the cadence has run out; FIRING → AIMING on that tick after one Shot; any state → DESTROYED at 0 hit points; DESTROYED → IDLE at `restore()`. The barrel turns `turn_rate × delta` a tick, clamped to the error, so a tracked aim point sits exactly on it. The Turret's `restore()` (story 012's, extended) also puts the head back at its rest yaw and calls `reset_physics_interpolation()` on it, because World draws physics transforms interpolated (`split_screen.tscn:47-48`) and the head would otherwise swing across one frame at R; it readies the cadence and returns to IDLE. The cadence counts the Turret's own ticks (`roundi(0.8 × Engine.physics_ticks_per_second)` = 48, the Weapon's rounding: `weapon.gd:86-88`), so the Round-over pause freezes it, which `Engine.get_physics_frames()` would not (`weapon.gd:82-88`). Its priority does not decide the double loss: a Turret destroys nothing, its Shot does, in the Shot's own tick under World, before the MatchController (`match_controller.gd:94-110`), whatever the Turret's priority (the critique corrects the brief here); one above the Units, as a Weapon (`weapon.gd:34-39`, `:76`), reads where the target stands after its move of the tick.

Never from the barrel's current muzzle: for a target beside or behind a resting barrel that reads up to 3.2 m (twice `muzzle_forward`) too far, and a Unit 34.5 m from the aimed muzzle and 60 degrees off a resting barrel would read 35.3 m, so the Turret would never wake for a target its Shot can reach. AC-2's check parks its Units so: from 45.6 degrees off the rest heading the resting muzzle reads over 35 m for a target 34.5 m from the aimed muzzle, so 50 degrees tells the two apart, and on Map 01 the salt flat in front of the canyon's south wall offers the place (computed: about (−77.8, 0, −17.7) at 34.5 m and (−77.2, 0, −18.4) at 35.5 m from Base A's ridge-side Turret at (−101, 0, 10), 50 degrees toward the canyon; the resting muzzle reads 35.09 m for the first, a thin margin to measure before pinning it). The canyon road no longer serves: its south wall now hides it (the sight ray, below). The canyon-side Turret reaches that Unit too, so break it first or filter the Shots by shooter. The cliffs are CSG shells (ConcavePolygonShape3D: `map_01_cliffs.tscn:8-49`), so measure first that the sight ray reports a cliff's face from outside, as story 007 measured the Gyrocopter's rise query (`kit_unit_model.gd:53-55`). The Flag has no body (`flag.gd:46-51`), so neither ray, nor a Turret's Shot, stops at it. On Map 01 the Gate walls do not hide every Flag Wall: each Turret sees, through the Gate, the half of its own Gate-side Flag Wall across the Gate's axis (story 012's `FlagWallGate`, x −1.7 to 1.7, outer face at z −9.3), so a Turret's Shot that misses a Unit just inside the Gate can end on it and takes nothing (the owner filter; AC-4; computed: from the Turret at (10, 0, −19), a Shot at a Unit at (2, 0, −12) that misses ends on it at about x −1.1). That panel alone also hides a narrow wedge of the yard, about 1.2 m wide, from the far Turret (computed: a Unit's centre at about (−7.6, 0, −3.9) from the Turret at (10, 0, −19)); the other three Flag Walls stand behind the Gate walls, and the depot's Fuel tanks are out of reach. The check that a Flag Wall hides a target may use that wedge or a panel placed in the open; no check may assert that no Flag Wall is in a Turret's sight.
- **The lead.** Aim at the target's position plus its velocity times the Shot's flight, solved for a target that holds its velocity (the intercept of its position P relative to the Turret's centre and its velocity V against the Shot's 60 m/s, which leaves `muzzle_forward` from the centre: |P + V·t| = `muzzle_forward` + 60·t), counting the ticks between the Turret's read and the Shot's first move (a Shot fired in one frame first moves in the next: `match_controller.gd:100-102`; measure it for a Turret's Shot before relying on it). The velocity is `get_real_velocity()`, the motion of the target's last move, not `current_speed`, which a Unit pinned against a wall keeps (`unit.gd:235-240`). An aim point more than 35 m from the aimed muzzle: hold fire. A model of the rule (computed, to be measured) hits with every Shot a Unit of any type crossing at top speed 10 to 30 m away, and with none of them without the lead; a Buggy circling at its tightest radius about 20 m away is hit by roughly a third.
- **Firing.** Instance the Turret's `shot_scene` (`combat/shot.tscn`, an export of the Turret) under World and call story 012's `launch_from()` in the same tick (`shot.gd:15-18` says why), with the Turret's RID, its `type_id`, its Player index, the muzzle (centre + barrel facing × `muzzle_forward` + up × `rules.shooting_height`, the Weapon's sum: `weapon.gd:102-104`; the aimed muzzle within the aim tolerance, since it fires only with the barrel on the aim), the barrel's facing and the TurretStats values. The Shot belongs to the `shots` group (`shot.tscn:13`), so `_free_shots()` already frees it at R (`split_screen.gd:192-198`). No matrix row: `damage_matrix.tres:7` gives every unlisted pair 1.0 (the script's own default is 0.0: `damage_matrix.gd:22-30`).
- **The hard rule.** `FlagRules._qualifies()` (`flag_rules.gd:140-150`) gains one gate, before the touch query: a Player who is not the Flag's owner does not qualify while a Structure of that Flag's Base (FlagRules holds the controller's Bases by reference: `flag_rules.gd:51-59`) stands and its stats say `locks_flag`. The owner's line (`:148`) is untouched, so an owner's dropped Flag is never locked. A Flag leaves its seat only to the other Player (`flag.gd:84-92`) and a Turret comes back only at R, so in play the gate only ever meets a Flag at home; a check can still drop a Flag by a standing Turret (`Flag.drop_at()`, as a tool may) to show the owner's exemption. A Shot that destroys the last Turret ticks before the controller, so a Motorbike already touching takes the Flag in that same frame (`match_controller.gd:290-300`). The HUD's Flag line keeps its three states (`match_controller.gd:220-229`).
