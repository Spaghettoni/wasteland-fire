**Layout and rules**

- [x] AC-1: Map 01 replaces the greybox field, the terrain stand-ins and the greybox Fuel Cans in the main scene. It is one Map scene laid out from the blueprint at 1 drawing metre = 1 world metre, north up (drawing x → world X = x − 150, drawing y → world Z = y − 50; north is −Z): the island inside its salt-water ring, the canyon with its road, the salt flat, the two fords, the southern ridge with its two channels, Base A in the west (Orange, Player 1) and Base B in the east (Teal, Player 2), the Fuel depot at the centre, the nine Fuel Can spots and the cover. Each sits within 1 m of its entry in the positions table (Implementation Notes), the reference for this check, except the canyon rock's north face, which is built out to the shore (open question 13). The table may be corrected before the build by re-measuring the 2640 px blueprint (x = (px − 120) / 8, y = (py − 200) / 8). The evidence doc lists each built position beside its table entry.
- [x] AC-2: Map 01 is mirror-symmetric about the blueprint's x = 150 (world X = 0), as the blueprint states: every collider, zone, `SpawnPoint` marker, Flag seat and Fuel Can spot in the east half mirrors one in the west half within 0.1 m, with mirrored headings. The evidence doc lists both halves. Colours are not part of this check.
- [x] AC-3: The salt water is the Map's edge and stops every Unit (blueprint legend: "Salt water — map edge, impassable"): ground Units at the shoreline (the cliffs and water layer of story 005), and the Gyrocopter at a boundary on the map layer along the shore, built from boxes at least 1 m thick, so no Unit leaves the island (open question 6). The two channels that cut the ridge south of the fords are salt water inside the Map, as the illustrated drawing paints them: they stop ground Units and the Gyrocopter crosses them (`design/rules.md`, Units). Nothing at the edge does damage. Checked with each of the four Units on the north, south, west and east shores and at a channel.
- [x] AC-4: The ridge is a cliff (blueprint legend: "Cliffs / ridge — gyrocopter only"; map label "RIDGE / AIR ONLY"), and the canyon walls are read as cliffs too (open question 14): both stop every ground Unit and the Gyrocopter crosses them, with story 005's layers; shots pass through them (cliffs and water are not in `shot_collision_mask` 19, so a shot at the shooting height of 0.5 m crosses rock and water as it crosses story 005's stand-ins: the canyon and the ridge give no cover from fire). Each ground Unit drives the canyon road (about 8 m wide as drawn) end to end, west to east and back, with throttle and steering only and no reverse (wall contact allowed), and the evidence doc records each run's time; the blueprint's white line along x = 150 is the seam between its mirrored halves, not a wall, so the road is open at X = 0. No ground Unit gets round the canyon on its north side: the rock is built out to the shore (open question 13). Checked with each of the four Units at both canyon walls, at the ridge and through the road.
- [x] AC-5: The fords slow ground Units (blueprint legend: "Shallow ford — creek, vehicles slowed"): inside either ford a ground Unit's speed is clamped to its type's top speed times one ford speed fraction, held as data in the Map (starting value 0.5, to tune by playing). The clamp is on the speed itself, because the movement model closes on a lower target only at the type's acceleration; the cap lifts on the first physics tick after the Unit leaves the ford, and the Unit then accelerates as usual. The Gyrocopter crosses at full speed (a reading, open question 2). Turning keeps the speed law of story 001 and Fuel burns per second as in story 006. A ford reports a Unit one to two physics ticks late (the zone latency story 006 measured), so the speed is asserted within 0.1 m/s from the fourth tick after the Unit's centre crosses into a ford to the fourth tick after it leaves, for the Motorbike, the Buggy and the Truck, and the Gyrocopter's full speed across one.
- [x] AC-6: Base A and Base B are walled compounds about 30 x 32 m as drawn, each with one gate about 10 m wide in the wall that faces the centre (blueprint legend: "Base (walled compound, gate faces center)"). The walls stop every Unit, the Gyrocopter included (open question 7), and every shot. Inside, the Garage stands at the back, open to the sky, with the Base's `SpawnPoint` marker and its two spares on its floor facing the gate (the blueprint's "Garage / respawn pad"); the Flag's seat is under the Base's water tank at its drawn centre, (−113, 0) and (113, 0) (blueprint: "Water tank = the flag"); the delivery zone is the blueprint's dotted line inside the walls. - [x] AC-7: The cover is static and never destroyed (blueprint legend: "Cover: wrecks & containers"; `design/game-brief.md`, Out of scope: no destructible buildings): the eight wrecks and the two long containers on the gate axis (read as the legend's containers), and the two scrap walls south of the depot, which the illustrated legend lists apart from cover ("Scrap wall") and which are built as cover too (open question 7). Checked with the Gyrocopter, a ground Unit and a shot at one wreck, one long container and one scrap wall.
