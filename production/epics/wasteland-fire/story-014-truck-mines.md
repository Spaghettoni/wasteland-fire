## Acceptance Criteria

*From the developer's request, the author's board card and the answer of 2026-10-07, scoped to this story:*

- [x] AC-1: A Player whose Unit is a Truck in play lays one Mine with one press of the lay key, E for Player 1 and Comma for Player 2: the Mine lies on the ground just behind the Truck's tail, clear of the Truck whether it stands or turns on the spot, and a key held down lays one Mine, not one per tick. A stranded Truck (no Fuel) lays like any other. The Motorbike, the Buggy and the Gyrocopter lay none: for them the key does nothing and shows nothing, as it does while the Player chooses or waits to respawn and while the Round is over.
- [x] AC-2: Every new Truck comes with 5 Mines: at the Round's first spawn, at every respawn and after every Swap at the own Base. Each Mine laid takes one; with none left a press lays nothing and that Player's notice line reads "No Mines left". While a Player's Unit is a Truck in play, that Player's view, and only that one, shows the Mines left as "Mines 5 / 5", updated on the tick of every lay and of every new Truck; the line is hidden for every other type and while the Player chooses or waits, fits the 640 x 720 view with nothing clipped, and covers no element of the HUD band, the choice panel, the respawn countdown, the out-of-Fuel hint or the notice line.
- [x] AC-3: A new Mine blinks in its owner's Team colour, Orange for Player 1 and Teal for Player 2, for 3 s and is harmless: no Unit on it, its own Truck included, sets it off. Then it shines steadily in that colour and is live. Both Players see every Mine, their own and the other Player's, in the view from above and in the chase view.
- [x] AC-4: A live Mine destroys at once every ground Unit on it, Motorbike, Buggy or Truck, its owner's included and a Truck at full hit points too: a destruction, not damage; a Unit still on a Mine when it goes live is destroyed the moment it does. Then the Mine is gone, with a short flash both Players see. The Gyrocopter never sets one off, hovering over it until its tank runs dry, crossing it at top speed or crashing on it; Shots fly over Mines, neither stopping on them nor setting them off; and a Unit that respawns after a Mine kill sets off no Mine at the place where it was destroyed.
- [x] AC-5: A lay is refused where any part of the Mine would lie inside either Base's walls (the yard and the Garage) or on the approach in front of either Gate, from Gate tower to Gate tower and about 10 m out (with the starting values no part of a Mine lies less than 9.9 m in front of the Gate wall's outer face; on Map 01 that refuses a drop point with world z from -15.5 to 15.5 and world x from -134.4 to -94.3 at Base A or from 94.3 to 134.4 at Base B), at the other Player's Base as at the own; and wherever the Mine would not lie on open ground: within its reach of a wall, a corner tower, the cover, a depot Fuel tank, a Turret, a Flag Wall, a cliff (a Truck backed flush against the rock, its drop point inside it, included) or water (on Map 01 every Turret and Flag Wall stands in a no-mine area, so there the Base's reason shows). A refused press lays nothing, keeps the count and shows that Player alone, for about 1.5 s on that Player's notice line, "No Mines in or near a Base" or "No room for a Mine here". Everywhere else on Map 01 a Truck lays: on the open ground, outside the Bases' side and back walls, in a ford, on a Fuel Can's spot, under a dropped Flag and on another Mine.
- [x] AC-6: A Mine kill is a destruction like any other: it costs one Token of the destroyed Unit's type; a Carrier destroyed on a Mine drops the Flag at the wreck, on the ground where a Motorbike can take it; a Mine that destroys a Player's last Motorbike ends the Round on that tick and the other Player wins; when the other Player's last Motorbike is destroyed on the same tick, by a Mine, a Shot or a Self-destruct, the Round ends once, with "Nobody wins!"; and a Unit that a Mine and a Shot take on the same tick, a Turret's Shot included, is destroyed once and costs one Token.
- [x] AC-7: Mines stay where they lie when their Truck is destroyed or swapped, still in its owner's colour and still live, and nothing caps how many lie on the Map. R (the restart) removes every Mine, arming, live or flashing, and the first Truck of the new Round comes with 5.
- [x] AC-8: The lay keys are Input Map actions, `p1_lay_mine` on E and `p2_lay_mine` on Comma (physical keys, like every other action), and the README's Controls table names them; the keyboard-ghosting tool gains the combinations that hold a lay key with the drive and fire keys, its self-test passes, and the real keyboard delivers them (a human check at `/story-done`).
- [x] AC-9: Every value is data: how many Mines a Unit type carries and where it drops them in its UnitStats `.tres` (5 and the drop point for the Truck, nothing for the other types); the arming time, the blink, the trigger's size, the approach's depth, the open-ground layers and probe, and the flash in a MineSettings `.tres`; the disc's thickness and height in `mine.tscn`; the keys in `project.godot`; the texts ("Mines %d / %d" and the three notices) in their scenes, through `tr()`, naming no key. No number is a constant in code. The evidence scenarios written before this story run with no Mines (no type carries any, and neither view has a Mines line) and keep their outputs: byte for byte, but for exact rewrites pinned in `normalizers.json` (the backtrace line numbers the new scenarios move in the runner, and the file and line counts of the HUD's code lint).

- **The data (AC-1, AC-2).** A `Mines` group in `UnitStats` after Fuel (`src/gameplay/units/unit_stats.gd:242-282`), with zero defaults by the file's rule (`:52-56`): `mine_capacity: int` (0 means the type lays none, the `fuel_capacity` pattern at `:244-250`, never a type check: `:147-153`) and `mine_drop_offset: Vector3` (local, behind the tail; the `muzzle_forward` analogue at `:187-191`; a Unit faces local -Z, `unit.gd:394`). Only `truck_stats.tres` gets lines: `mine_capacity = 5` and `mine_drop_offset = Vector3(0, 0, 3.4)`. The Truck's box is 2.4 x 4.4 m (`truck_stats.tres:27-28`), so its tail is 2.2 m behind its origin and its corners 2.51 m from it: a 0.8 m trigger at 3.4 m clears the standing Truck by 0.4 m and one turning on the spot by 0.09 m. `unit.gd` is not touched (761 lines, TD-010): the count per Truck lives in the MineLayer.
- **MineSettings** (`src/gameplay/mines/mine_settings.gd` and `data/mine_settings.tres`), the `FordSettings` and `FuelCanSettings` pattern (`ford_settings.gd:13-25`; `fuel_can_settings.gd:14-30`): zero defaults, read-only at runtime, a `first_problem()` like `UnitStats.first_problem()` (`unit_stats.gd:285-297`). Starting values: arming 3.0 s (board), a blink period of 0.5 s, trigger radius 0.8 m, `approach_past_zone` 11.0 m (how far the no-mine approach reaches past the Base zone's Gate-side face, which on Map 01 lies 1.1 m inside the Gate wall's outer face; below), the open-ground mask 97 (map 1 + cliffs_water 32 + cover 64: `project.godot:118-124`), the open-ground probe from 0.1 m to 20.0 m above the drop point (below), a flash of 0.3 s. Every duration is counted in the Mine's own ticks, a counter its `_physics_process` raises, so the Round-over pause freezes it; the Weapon's stamp, `Engine.get_physics_frames()`, keeps counting under the pause (`weapon.gd:82-88`; `garage_queue.gd:111-112`). Each duration is rounded as the Weapon rounds its cadence, `roundi(seconds * Engine.physics_ticks_per_second)` (`weapon.gd:86-87`). `mine.tscn` and both MineLayers reference the one `.tres`.
- **Mine** (`src/gameplay/mines/mine.gd` and `mine.tscn`, `class_name Mine`): an Area3D on no physics layer that watches mask 2 alone, the Ford's setup (`ford.tscn:14-16`; `ford.gd:27-29`), so it lists ground Units and never the Gyrocopter (layer 16) or a benched or destroyed Unit (layer 0), with no type check; a Shot's ray tests bodies only (`shot.gd:72-78`), so Shots fly through. Its trigger shape is its own, sized from `trigger_radius` (a new shape, never a write to the scene's shared one, as `Unit._apply_type()` makes its box: `unit.gd:705-713`), with a Body disc as wide as the trigger (what a Player sees is what sets it off) whose `material_override` is the laying Unit's `team_material` (`unit.gd:227-233`) and whose `cast_shadow` is off like the other flat pieces (`shot.tscn:17`). The disc is a thin cylinder of unit radius stored in `mine.tscn`, and `_ready()` scales the Body node on x and z to `trigger_radius`, so a retuned trigger redraws the disc while every Mine still shares the one mesh (sharing survives node scale: `production/qa/evidence/story-007-the-map/measurements/look-preflight-report.txt:187-188`). Its top stands at least 0.03 m over the drop point (its height in the scene): the drop point is on the floor at y 0, the salt flat and the canyon road are drawn at 0.01 m (`map_01_terrain.tscn:116-125`) and a ford's water at 0.02 m (`ford.tscn:24-26`), so a disc at the floor would be drawn under them, and 10 mm keeps one flat surface clear of another (`production/qa/evidence/story-007-the-map/measurements/layout-preflight-report.txt:333`). The Flash beside it is unshaded, in the Shot tracer's pale yellow (`shot.tscn:5-7`), on render layer 1 like the disc, which both views draw (the view from above hides layer 2 only: `above_camera_settings.tres:12`). Every Mine shares one disc mesh and its Player's one material, so a field of Mines stays cheap: a mesh of its own per instance costs about 2.2 draw calls across both views, a shared one far less (`look-preflight-report.txt:185-190`); no frame-rate run is required (story 013's `turrets_fps` takes the frame rate with the Flag Walls and the Turrets; neither it nor `map_fps` lays a Mine). The root sits in the group `"mines"`, stored in the scene as `shot.tscn:13` stores `"shots"`. States, with the complete transition table `.claude/rules/gameplay-code.md` asks for: ARMING (blinking, harmless) -> LIVE (steady) -> SPENT (the flash, then `queue_free()`).
- **The Mine's tick (AC-3, AC-4).** Polled with `get_overlapping_bodies()`, never `body_entered` (`ford.gd:22-25`; `fuel_can.gd:22-27`), noting the list on every tick, ARMING included, as a taken Fuel Can does (`fuel_can.gd:26-27`). Once LIVE, every Unit the zone lists on this tick and listed on the Mine's previous tick (`fuel_can.gd:29-34`: a respawned Unit is reported once more at its old place) and that is alive (`:40-42`: a destroyed Unit stays listed for a tick) gets `Unit.destroy()` (`unit.gd:478-497`: at once, whatever its hit points; `apply_damage()` through the matrix would leave a 220 hp Truck alive, against the board's "even a Truck"), in node-name order so a two-Unit blast reads the same in every run (the engine's list order is not reproducible: `fuel_can.gd:34-39`); then SPENT. A LIVE Mine whose listed Units are all dead stays LIVE, so stacked Mines spend one per Unit.
- **The frame order (AC-6).** `destroy()` is called from the Mine's own `_physics_process`, never from a signal handler: the `destroyed` handler drops a Carrier's Flag by reparenting it (`flag_rules.gd:33-40`; `unit.gd:441-450`). The Mines are World's children, added after the Units (`unit.get_parent()`, as `weapon.gd:113`), so at the default priority 0 they tick after the Units and before the MatchController (`match_controller.gd:94-110`; World comes first: `split_screen.tscn:47, 244`): a Mine's destruction is in the ledger when that frame's loss check reads it (`:589-600`), and a Mine with a priority above 0 would split a double loss over two frames (`:106-110`). The MineLayer and PlayerMineInput destroy nothing, so their priorities do not matter to the loss (the critique's correction for the Turrets holds here too). New code polls `is_alive` and connects nothing to `Unit.destroyed`: `tokens_data` prints its links (`tokens_data_kit.gd:113-115`).
- **Measure the latency first (AC-4).** A zone lists a Unit a tick after its box first overlaps it, and a zone that ticks after the Units bites a tick later than one before them (`ford.gd:31-40`); a spawned Unit is listed two ticks later (`fuel_can.gd:45-48`); the two-tick rule adds one more. Expect the blast three or four ticks after the box first touches the trigger (a Motorbike at 24 m/s moves 0.4 m a tick), but measure it with the `zone_latency` pattern (`tools/evidence/split_screen/unit_swap_kit.gd:146-155`) before any check pins a tick, and record the side offset at which a Motorbike at top speed grazes past without a blast.
- **MineLayer** (`src/gameplay/mines/mine_layer.gd`), one per Player under World in `split_screen.tscn`, beside the Weapons (`:87-99`): exports `unit`, `settings` and `mine_scene`, and no Player index of its own: where one is needed (the Mine's record of its owner) it reads `unit.player_index` (Story 012), never a second index stored in the scene, which could disagree in silence (critique). The count per Truck: refilled to `unit.stats.mine_capacity` on the first tick it finds its Unit alive after a tick it was not; the Round never spawns a Unit still in play (`GarageQueue.tick()` spawns only a WAITING Player, and never on the tick of its bench or its destruction: `garage_queue.gd:221-231, 262-278, 286-294`), so every spawn, Swap and respawn is seen.
