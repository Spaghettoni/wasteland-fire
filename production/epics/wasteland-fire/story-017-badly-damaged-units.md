## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 2 and the answer of 2026-10-10, scoped to this story:*

- [ ] AC-1: A live Unit of each of the four types is badly damaged while its hit points are at or below its type's badly damaged fraction of its own maximum: with the starting 0.2, at 8 of 40 or fewer for the Motorbike, 20 of 100 for the Buggy, 44 of 220 for the Truck and 14 of 70 for the Gyrocopter. The edge belongs to the state: a Buggy left at exactly 20 by 16 Motorbike Shots is badly damaged and one at 25 after 15 is not; likewise a Motorbike at 5 after seven Motorbike Shots but not at 10 after six, a Truck at 20 after eight Truck Shots but not at 45 after seven, and a Gyrocopter at 10 after twelve Motorbike Shots but not at 15 after eleven. The state begins on the tick of the hit that brings the Unit there, a Turret's Shot included, and lasts until the Unit is destroyed or benched (a Swap at the own Base, or R): no hit point comes back and nothing else ends it. The Unit that comes next, at a respawn, after a Swap or after R, starts with full hit points and out of the state. A Mine, a Self-destruct and the Gyrocopter's Fuel crash destroy at once and pass through no state.
- [ ] AC-2: While badly damaged, a Unit's top speed is its type's speed share of its own, forward and in reverse: with the starting 0.5, Motorbike 12 and 4.5 m/s, Buggy 9.8 and 4.0, Truck 5.45 and 2.5, Gyrocopter 8.2 and 3.0 (half of 24 and 9, 19.6 and 8, 10.9 and 5, 16.4 and 6). A Unit going faster when the state begins slows to it at its own acceleration, with no jolt: from full speed in about 0.6 s for every type (Motorbike 0.60 s, Buggy 0.58, Truck 0.61, Gyrocopter 0.55; the check measures the ticks). Nothing else of its driving changes: acceleration, braking and coasting, the turn rates (story 009's turn on the spot included), the weapon (damage, rate, range, Shot speed), the Fuel burn per second and the hit-point rules; a stranded badly damaged ground Unit still turns and fires. The HUD shows what it showed ("HP n / max", the hit-point bar, the Fuel gauge) with nothing added.
- [ ] AC-3: In a ford the lower cap wins. With the starting values both are a half (the ford's fraction is 0.5), so a badly damaged ground Unit crosses a ford at half its top speed, as an undamaged one does there, never at a quarter; with a share below the ford's fraction (0.4, in a copy of the data the check makes) the share holds inside the ford too, and with one above it (0.6) the ford's fraction does. Out of the ford a badly damaged Unit accelerates back only to its share. The Gyrocopter, which no ford slows, holds its share everywhere.
- [ ] AC-4: While a Unit is badly damaged, thin dark grey smoke rises from its drawn body and trails behind it while it moves (a column while it stands), the same for all four types, with no Team colour and no shadow. Both Players see it on both Units, in the view from above and in the chase view; a Gyrocopter's smoke rises from its drawn model, at its hover and over the cliffs and the cover its model rises over, never from the floor under it. The smoke shows from the tick the state begins, never on a Unit that is not badly damaged, and is gone on the tick the Unit is destroyed or benched: none hangs over the wreck, and that Player's next Unit shows none. Seen from above, the smoke leaves the Unit's Team accent readable (a human check). Added for coherence: with both Units smoking, measured windowed at 1280 x 720 the `map_fps` way, the frame stays within the project budget: the lowest one-second rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms and at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together (`.claude/docs/technical-preferences.md:41-43`; story 013 AC-8). The measured rate and the draw calls the smoke adds in each view are recorded as observations beside story 012's 222 and 223 (`production/qa/evidence/story-012-flag-walls-evidence.md:20`), never as thresholds.
- [ ] AC-5 (added for coherence with story 016): A badly damaged Unit that is destroyed, by a Unit's Shot, a Turret's Shot, a Mine, a Self-destruct or the Gyrocopter's Fuel crash, explodes as story 016 built, and its own smoke ends on that tick, leaving only the explosion's; a badly damaged Unit put away by a Swap at the own Base does not explode, and the new Unit appears at full hit points, at full speed and with no smoke (the Swap's free repair, `design/rules.md`, "Destruction and respawn"). A destruction still costs one Token, and a badly damaged Carrier still drops the Flag at the wreck.
- [ ] AC-6: Nothing else changes: `turret.gd`, `shot.gd`, `ford.gd`, `match_controller.gd` and `player_hud.gd` are not touched, so the Turrets aim, lead and fire as story 013 built them, the ford slows as story 007 built it and the HUD is as it was. The consequence for the Turrets is recorded, not changed: `badly_damaged` measures, pinning no outcome, how many of a Turret's Shots hit a Buggy driven across its front at full speed and the same Buggy badly damaged at its share, and the evidence states it for the author.
- [ ] AC-7: Every value is data: the badly damaged fraction and the speed share of each type in its UnitStats `.tres` (0.2 and 0.5 in each of the four files; a fraction of 0 means the type has no such state, and a type whose `.tres` has no line has none), refused by `UnitStats.first_problem()` when the fraction lies outside 0 to under 1 or, while the fraction is above 0, the share outside above 0 to 1, a NaN included; the smoke's look (amount, lifetime, rise, size, colour, fade) in its scene; where it rises from given by the model (the top of its hull), not by a new number. No number is a constant in code. The evidence scenarios written before this story, every one that does not declare `BADLY_DAMAGED`, run with no badly damaged state (every type's fraction 0 by data, so no Unit's speed changes, nothing new is emitted and no smoke node ever exists) and keep their outputs byte for byte against the capture taken at the start of the story, but for exact rewrites pinned in `normalizers.json` (the script lines their backtraces print that this story moves, `unit.gd`'s `destroyed.emit()` frame and the runner's, and any lint whose file and line counts grow).
