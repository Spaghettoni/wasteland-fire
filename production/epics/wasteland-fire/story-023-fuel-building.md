## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 8 and the developer's answers of 2026-10-10, scoped to this story:*

- [x] AC-1: The nine Fuel Cans are gone from Map 01, the two in the canyon, the two on the salt flat and the five in the depot, and with them their respawn and their return at R: nothing on Map 01 gives Fuel but the Fuel building's zones, and a Unit parked on any of the nine old spots gains nothing.
- [x] AC-2: The Fuel building is the depot at the centre of Map 01, its three Fuel tanks as before (Tall pieces: they stop every Unit and every Shot), with one Fuel zone on each of its two Base-facing sides, the west one facing Base A and the east one facing Base B, mirrored across x = 0, each right in front of the depot and reachable by a ground Unit without touching a tank. Nothing damages the depot or its zones. Each zone is drawn on the ground so a Player sees where to stop, in both Players' views and in both camera views, and the marked ground reads as a Fuel place by more than its colour.
- [x] AC-3: A Unit that has stopped in a Fuel zone refuels gradually, from empty to full in about 4 s (net of the Unit's own standing burn), up to full and no further, its Fuel bar rising as it fills; a Fuel zone gives Fuel only (no ammo, no Mines, no hit points). Stopped means the Unit's real speed is about zero: a Unit held against a tank or a parked Unit with the throttle on refuels, while one rolling through at any speed does not, and one that moves off or leaves the zone stops refuelling within the measured latency. Whether a parked Unit that another Unit drives into refuels is measured and recorded, with no outcome fixed (a CharacterBody3D is moved only by its own `move_and_slide()`). Units of both Players refuel at the same zone on the same ticks, and a zone never runs dry and holds nothing R must restore.
- [x] AC-4: A Gyrocopter hovering still over a Fuel zone at its flying height refuels as a ground Unit does; it reaches each zone from its own Base's side without touching a Tall piece (no crash: story 021), and while it hovers in a Fuel zone its tank never runs dry.
- [x] AC-5: A ground Unit that runs out of Fuel anywhere but in a Fuel zone stays stranded: nothing on the Map refuels it, and its Player's view shows the out-of-Fuel hint offering the Self-destruct (inside the own Base, the Swap) as before, which brings a new Unit with full supplies (story 022). One that runs dry while stopped in a Fuel zone is refuelled there, its hint goes, and it drives on.
- [x] AC-6: Every value is data: the time from empty to full (4 s) and the speed below which a Unit counts as stopped in a SupplySettings `.tres`; each zone's size, height and kind in its scene; the zones' places in `map_01_depot.tscn`; the look in the scenes. No number is a constant in code. The evidence scenarios written before this story run on the Map they were measured on (the nine Fuel Cans in their places with their return at R, no Fuel zone) and keep their outputs byte for byte against the baseline captured at the start of the story, but for exact rewrites pinned in `normalizers.json`; each strip is proven by a seeded "runner leaves it on" defect. The frame rate with the zones drawn stays within the project budget: the lowest one-second rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms, at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together.
