## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 3, the developer's answers A1 and A10 and the readings R10, R11 and R18 of 2026-10-10, scoped to the walls of both Bases:*

- [ ] AC-1: Each Base on Map 01 has its walls as Wall Panels: 8 along the back wall, 8 along each side wall and 3 in each Gate wall, 30 per Base and 60 on the Map, each 2 m tall (its collider from 0 to 2 m) and 1.3 m thick like the walls they replace, fitted end to end and flush, with no gap and no overlap, between the corner towers' inner faces and the Gate posts, in the old walls' footprint (Base-local: the back wall from x -13.85 to 13.85 at z 13.7 to 15.0, each panel 3.4625 m long; each side wall from z -12.85 to 12.85 at |x| 14.7 to 16.0, each 3.2125 m; each Gate wall from |x| 5.5 to 13.85 at z -15.0 to -13.7, each 2.783 m), mirror images across x = 0, dark like the Flag Walls. The four corner towers of each Base stand as they are (4.3 x 6 x 4.3 m, in the Team colour, solid to every Unit and every Shot). The Gate stays the 11 m opening between the Gate walls (|x| below 5.5), and the Base zone (29.4 x 4 x 27.5 m), the Garage with its walls and spawn spots, the box of Flag Walls, the water tower and the Turrets stand where they stood: from either spawn spot of either Base every type still drives straight out through the Gate on the throttle alone, touching no Flag Wall, leg, Gate post or panel (story 012 AC-6).
- [ ] AC-2: A standing Wall Panel stops what a Flag Wall stops: a Motorbike, a Buggy or a Truck driven head-on at full throttle into a panel of each of the five runs, from the yard and from outside, stops flush at its face as at a wall; a Shot of any type (Space for Player 1, Period for Player 2) and a Turret's Shot whose path meets a standing panel end on it; a Turret's sight to a target behind one is blocked. The Gyrocopter flies over every run at full speed without slowing, into either yard and out again, its model rising over each panel and settling back as over a Flag Wall (until story 020 fixes one flying height); the corner towers, the water tower's legs and the Garage walls (story 019's) still stop it. Nothing that watches Units sees a panel: the Base zone, the Flag's touch and the test for a free spawn spot ignore it.
- [ ] AC-3: A Wall Panel has 40 hit points. A Shot of the other Player that ends on one takes the shooter's damage times the damage matrix's multiplier off it: x1.0 for the Buggy (12 a Shot), the Truck (25) and the Gyrocopter (15), so a panel falls to the 4th Buggy Shot, the 2nd Truck Shot or the 3rd Gyrocopter Shot, and x0.0 for the Motorbike, whose Shot ends on the panel and takes nothing, however long it fires. A Player's own Shots of every type, and that Player's own Turrets' Shots, end on that Player's own Base's panels and take nothing. While it stands a panel shows the Flag Walls' looks: whole while it keeps 80% of its hit points or more, its first damaged look below 80% (darker and cracked, a few chunks at its foot) and its second below 35% (darker still, more chunks), exactly one look at a time, and at exactly 80% or 35% the look above. Only the look changes: the collider, the physics layer, the height and what stops at a panel stay those of the whole panel until it falls, and the chunks have no collider.
- [ ] AC-4: A panel whose hit points reach 0 falls into low rubble on that tick and from that tick blocks nothing (story 012 measured a fallen Flag Wall clear to a ray in the tick it fell): a ground Unit drives through the gap and a Shot flies through it, while its neighbours stand. One fallen panel lets every ground type through: a Truck (2.4 m wide, the widest) drives straight through the 2.783 m breach of a single Gate-wall panel, and a Motorbike, a Buggy and a Truck through the breach of one panel of the back wall and of each side wall. A breach is not a Gate (R11): nothing about the Base changes when a panel falls. The Base zone, the Swap inside it (Tab or Enter), a delivery, the Turrets' Flag lock and the spawn spots work as before, so a Carrier with the other Player's Flag that drives into its own yard through a breach delivers the moment its zone reports it, as through the Gate.
- [ ] AC-5: With the Gate-wall panel in front of a Turret standing, the other Player's Unit parked in the yard behind it, within the Turret's 35 m range and where the sight line crosses that panel, is neither seen nor fired at by that Turret; with the panel fallen the Turret sees it through the breach, turns on it and fires, and its Shot reaches the Unit; after R the panel hides it again. At both Bases, and for both Turrets of each.
- [ ] AC-6: The no-Mine area does not change (decided 2026-10-07: it is the Base zone and the approach in front of the Gate, grown by the trigger radius, and does not follow the walls): with the panels standing or fallen, a lay whose drop point lies at Base-local |x| up to 15.5 and z from -25.7 to 14.4 at either Base is refused with "No Mines in or near a Base", so every point of a Gate wall's breach is refused. Outside that area, a drop point within a Mine's reach of a standing panel is refused with "No room for a Mine here"; once that panel has fallen the ground in front of it is open and a Truck lays there, in the outer part of a side or back wall's breach (a drop point from Base-local |x| 15.5 outward along a side wall or from z 14.4 outward along the back wall, at least 0.8 m from the ends of the standing neighbours; on Map 01 from world |z| 15.5 outward at x -132.85 to -107.15 at Base A or 107.15 to 132.85 at Base B along a side wall, and from world |x| 134.4 outward at |z| up to 13.85 along a back wall). A Mine laid there is like any other live Mine: it destroys the ground Unit that drives through the breach over it, and R removes it (story 014 AC-7) on the restart that brings the panel back.
- [ ] AC-7: A fallen panel stays down for the rest of the Round: no key or rule builds, repairs or captures one, and no destruction, respawn, Swap or Flag drop brings it back; a hurt one keeps its look until R. When the Round is over, R ("Press R to play again") brings back every Wall Panel of both Bases on the restart, with 40 hit points, solid, its whole look shown and the rubble gone, after every Flag is seated and both Units are benched, so a panel never closes on a Unit; the next Round starts with both compounds closed but for their Gates.
- [ ] AC-8: Retained windowed frames show both Bases whole, with panels at each damaged look, and breached (a fallen panel in the back wall, a side wall and a Gate wall), in the chase view and in the view from above (the list is under Test Evidence). `map_fps` and `turrets_fps`, run windowed with the panels in (every panel whole; every panel at its second damaged look; every panel fallen), stay within the project's budget (`.claude/docs/technical-preferences.md:41-43`; story 013 AC-8): the lowest one-second frame rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms, and at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together, the provisional budget. The measured rates and `draw_calls_max` are recorded as observations beside story 012's (`fps_min_steady` 119 and `fps_avg_steady` 120 on the dev machine's 120 Hz display; `draw_calls_max` 222 without and 223 with the Flag Walls: `production/qa/evidence/story-012-flag-walls-evidence.md:20`), never as thresholds.
- [ ] AC-9: Every value is data: the panels' size, physics layer (64, the cover's), mask (0) and looks in their scene or scenes; the 60 places in `compound_base.tscn`, Base B's mirrored by its transform in `map_01.tscn`; the hit points, the type id and the two fractions in the stats `.tres` (the Flag Walls' `flag_wall_stats.tres`, or a `.tres` of their own whose type id keeps the Motorbike's 0.0: R10, the build's call); no number and no Unit type is written in code, and nothing new is shown as text. Every evidence scenario that does not declare this story's opt-in `WALL_PANELS`, each one written before this story among them, runs with the old 3.2 m walls on the map layer, put back from a frozen copy, and every output of this story's regression baseline (a capture taken twice at the start of the story; Implementation Notes, The older outputs) stays byte-identical (R18), but for exact rewrites pinned in `normalizers.json`.
