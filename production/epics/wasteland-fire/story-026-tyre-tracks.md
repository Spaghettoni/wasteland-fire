## Context

**GDD**: `design/game-brief.md`, `design/rules.md` ("Teams and visual style"; "Units", for the types)
**Requirement**: The developer's request of 2026-10-15, made in a session: "And I also want ground vehicles to leave tire tracks behind. Perhaps Terrain3D package can be used for that?" No card of the author's board was named for it (the developer chose to draft the stories, 2026-10-15). The developer's answers of the same day (decided 2026-10-15): the tracks are drawn by the Map's ground itself from a track map, one texture over the Map that every tyre paints as it rolls and that the ground's shader reads, and not by Terrain3D or by ribbon meshes; and each mark fades out over about a minute.

Why not Terrain3D (the developer's question, answered 2026-10-15): it paints one pixel per terrain vertex, 256 pixels across a 256 m region by default ("a vertex painter, not a pixel painter"), so a tyre of 0.20 to 0.36 m would paint a smudge about 1 m wide. It has no tracks feature, so the tracks code would be written anyway. Its latest release, 1.0.2, supports Godot 4.4 to 4.6 "and possibly later", so 4.7.2 is not confirmed. It is a native plugin the Windows build would have to ship. And Map 01's ground, a flat island of CSG shapes and a plane drawn by the repository's own shader, would be rebuilt in its texture system. Sources: the Terrain3D documentation at `terrain3d.readthedocs.io` (Installation, Introduction, Terrain3DData), read 2026-10-15. It is worth a new look if a Map ever needs hills. Ribbon meshes behind each wheel were offered and not taken: crisper up close, but only the last stretch exists and they draw on whatever is under the wheel.

## Acceptance Criteria

*From the developer's request and answers of 2026-10-15, scoped to this story:*

- [x] AC-1: A ground Unit (Motorbike, Buggy, Truck) leaves a track under each tyre while it rolls on the ground, forward or in reverse, as wide as that tyre (data: the Motorbike's 0.20 m front and 0.25 m rear, the Buggy's 0.30 m, the Truck's 0.36 m). The Motorbike leaves one line (its two wheels are in line), the Buggy and the Truck two: a Unit turns about its middle (`unit.gd:470-484`), so in a steady turn the front and rear tyres of a side run on one line, and a turn on the spot leaves a ring. The Gyrocopter leaves none.
- [x] AC-2: The look: a track is darker, packed ground with a shallow rut that catches the low sun, readable from the chase view and from above. It shows on the sand, the canyon road and the salt flat (the three materials of `spatial_env_ground.gdshader`), on their level faces only; never on water, the fords, the channels, the Base pads, the Garage floors, the Island's shore edge or anything standing on the ground. Two tracks that cross are no darker than one.
- [x] AC-3: A mark keeps its full strength for a hold time and then fades to nothing over a fade time (data: 20 s and 40 s to start, about a minute in all), counted in the Round's time, so a fresh track reads stronger than an old one. The Round-over pause stops the fading with everything else; the next Round, and R, start on clean ground.
- [x] AC-4: Never ahead and never a jump: at 120 fps windowed a track ends at or behind its drawn tyre; a spawn, a respawn, a Swap or a bench never draws a line from where the Unit was to where it appears; a destroyed or benched Unit leaves nothing.
- [x] AC-5: The cost does not grow with what has been driven: one track texture for the Map (about 4096 x 1284 pixels for Map 01's 294 x 92.2 m island, about 7 cm a pixel: data), drawn into only where tyres rolled since the last frame. With both Units driving for a whole Round, the frame stays within the project budget, measured windowed at 1280 x 720 the `map_fps` way: the lowest one-second rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms and at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together. The GPU time with and without tracks is recorded as an observation beside TD-016 and TD-017, and the ground shader's added texture reads are named in its Budget line. *(Accepted by the developer on 2026-10-16: the lowest one-second rate (117 to 120 fps against 60), the draw calls (379 against 1,000), the cost not growing with what was driven and the Budget line hold; the clause 'a frame at most 16.6 ms' is exceeded by single frames in steady seconds (25.14 and 30.82 ms in 2 of 3 runs with the tracks) that also appear without the tracks node (33.52 ms in 1 of 3) and on the tree before the story (17.77 and 18.82 ms in 2 of 3), the pattern TD-016 holds, while the tracks add about 0.06 ms of GPU a frame; no new debt row, see the Completion Notes.)*
- [x] AC-6: Every value is data: the Map's track area and the texture's size (in the Map scene); each type's tyre contacts and widths (where its tyres touch the ground in the model's frame, from the Blender scripts; on its model scene or its UnitStats, the build's call); the look (darkness, rut depth, roughness); the hold and the fade; and the longest step a tyre may make in one tick before it counts as a jump. No number in code but 0 and 1.
- [x] AC-7: The Map contract: a Map with no tracks node, or whose ground is not drawn by `spatial_env_ground.gdshader`, shows no tracks and logs nothing (the greybox field of the older scenarios, the driving toy). SplitScreen hands the tracks node the two Units before the Round begins, from its own exports, with no search of the tree and no singleton (the `_hand_supply_zones()` way).
- [x] AC-8: The older evidence scenarios keep their outputs byte for byte against the capture taken at the start of the story: every scenario that does not declare the new opt-in runs with no tracks node.

## Implementation Notes

*At `minimal` there is no ADR; these are pointers, not decisions. The file:line pointers are `main` at 14eec71: re-find them as story 025 leaves them if it is built first.*

- **What 14eec71 gives.** Map 01's ground is three flat surfaces at about y 0 (`src/gameplay/maps/map_01/map_01_terrain.tscn:109-126`): the Island, a CSGPolygon3D spanning x ±147 m and z ±46.1 m (`:112`), in `materials/sand.tres`; the CanyonRoad, a CSGPolygon3D, in `road.tres`; and the SaltFlat, a 190 x 36 m plane, in `salt_flat.tres`. All three are ShaderMaterials of `src/gameplay/maps/spatial_env_ground.gdshader`, which samples everything in world space from `world_position.xz` (`:156`) and blends level faces from side faces by `level` (the shore edge: `:195-196`, `:204`). The fords, the channels and the Garage floors are StandardMaterial3D (`materials/ford.tres`, `channel.tres`, `garage_floor.tres`), so a map that the ground shader reads shows on the three grounds and nowhere else, with no list of surfaces (AC-2). The Map is World's first child, so a Map node that acts on the Units in its physics tick runs before they move, every tick (`src/gameplay/split_screen/split_screen.gd:46-47`). A tracks node there reads where each tyre stood at the end of the previous tick, one tick behind the body, which keeps a track behind the drawn tyre while World's interpolation draws the model between ticks (AC-4; measure first, windowed at 120 fps).
- **The tyres.** From the Blender scripts (`tools/blender/build_motorbike.py:28-34`, `build_buggy.py:28-33`, `build_truck.py:28-33`). A Blender point (x, y, z) is (x, z, -y) in Godot, and the three mesh model scenes set no `model_scale` or `offset`, so the model's frame is the Unit's. In x and z, metres, forward being -z: the Motorbike's front tyre at (0, -0.76), 0.20 m wide, and its rear at (0, 0.72), 0.25 m; the Buggy's four at (±0.74, -0.92) and (±0.74, 0.92), 0.30 m; the Truck's four at (±0.86, -1.28) and (±0.86, 1.30), 0.36 m. The Gyrocopter's model lists none, so it leaves no tracks with no type check (AC-1). A rebuilt model changes these numbers by hand, so a check compares them with the scripts' constants.
- **The strokes** (a `TyreTracks` node in the Map, say `src/gameplay/maps/tyre_tracks.gd`; MapField gains an export for it, as it has `supply_zones`: `map_field.gd:38-43`). On each physics tick, for each Unit it was handed and each of its tyres, the node takes the contact in the world (the Unit's global transform times the contact). It draws no stroke while the Unit is not alive, is not on the floor (`is_on_floor()`), was not alive the tick before (a spawn), or when the contact moved farther than the jump step since the last tick (a teleport); otherwise it adds a stroke from the last contact to this one, at the tyre's width, stamped with the Round's time. A Unit that turns on the spot moves its contacts along a circle about its middle, which makes strokes too. There are 60 physics ticks a second, so at the Motorbike's 12 m/s a stroke is 0.2 m long. Each tick's stroke list is the node's logic: it runs headless and is what the evidence checks. The drawing below is a picture that only a windowed run shows.
- **The track map** (measure first, Godot 4.7.2). A SubViewport for 2D only (`disable_3d`), its size the Map's data, holding a Node2D that draws the frame's new strokes in `_draw()`: `draw_line()` at the tyre's width in pixels, or quads. Since 4.7, lines no longer get the antialiasing feather (`docs/engine-reference/godot/modules/rendering.md:15`). Set `render_target_clear_mode` to CLEAR_MODE_ONCE at the start and at every new Round, and NEVER in between ("intended for 2D usage", per the 4.7 SubViewport reference), so what was drawn stays; set `render_target_update_mode` to UPDATE_ONCE on a frame with new strokes, or ALWAYS (measure the cost of each). Use two channels: coverage (R, 1 under a stroke) and the stroke's time (G, the Round's time in seconds plus an offset, so a cleared texel reads as long faded). A float buffer through `Viewport.use_hdr_2d` holds a time over 1: measure first that a value over 1 survives in the texture, by reading back `get_texture().get_image()` windowed. If the SubViewport cannot hold it, the alternatives to measure are an 8-bit fade that subtracts a step of coverage over the whole map at a fixed interval (one full-texture draw every few frames), or `DrawableTexture2D` (new in 4.7: `setup()` then `blit_rect()`, marked experimental; `rendering.md:21`).
- **The ground shader.** Three global uniforms, declared in `project.godot`'s `[shader_globals]` (4.7: "the global uniform *must* exist in the Project Settings at the time the shader is saved, or compilation will fail"): the track texture, the track area (origin and size in metres on X and Z) and the time now. Add a strength that defaults to 0, so a Map with no tracks node shows none (AC-7) even though every ground material reads the uniforms. They are set at runtime with `RenderingServer.global_shader_parameter_set()`. Whether a global sampler accepts a filter hint is not in the 4.7 reference: measure. In the fragment (`spatial_env_ground.gdshader:155`), read the map at `(p - origin) / size`; the track's strength is coverage times the fade of (now − time) through the hold and the fade, times `level` (no tracks on side faces). The albedo darkens by the track's darkness (data), the roughness changes by its own amount, and the rut is a slope from the map's coverage one texel to either side on X and Z (two to four more reads), added to `slope` before `relief` (`:200-203`). A SubViewport texture has no mipmaps (the 4.7 reference names none), so far from the chase camera the tracks may shimmer: measure, and if they do, fade them by the texel's size on screen (`fwidth` of the map's UV). Update the Budget line (`:22`), ten reads today.
- **Hand-off and restarts.** SplitScreen hands the tracks node the two Units before the Round begins (beside `_hand_supply_zones()`: `split_screen.gd:325`) and connects `round_started` to its clear, which the restarts emit (`:254-260`). The node sets the global uniforms when it enters the tree and the strength to 0 when it leaves, so a scene change leaves no tracks behind. The Round-over pause stops the node's tick, so the time and the fade stop with it (AC-3).
- **The older outputs** (AC-8). `map_layout` prints the Map's scripts (its allowlist), so the node would show in the older outputs. The runner (`tools/evidence/split_screen_harness.gd`) therefore gets a new opt-in, `const TYRE_TRACKS: bool = true`, and removes the node from the Map before `add_child()` for every other scenario, beside the other scene strips; the shader's uniforms exist always and are inert headless. Measure first by running one older Map 01 scenario with the strip off and diffing its output. The baseline is story 024's 75 retained outputs, plus story 025's if it was built first, captured twice before the first edit; grep every retained output for `\.gd:[0-9]+` and plan an exact rewrite in `measurements/normalizers.json` for each line this story moves (`split_screen.gd`, `map_field.gd` and the runner).
- **The evidence scenarios.** `tyre_tracks` (headless, run twice byte-identical, declaring `TYRE_TRACKS` with the opt-ins `ammo_buildings` declares) checks: for each type, the strokes of a straight run, a turn, a turn on the spot and a reverse, against the tyres' places and widths (one line for the Motorbike, two for the Buggy and the Truck, the front and rear tyres of a side on one line in a steady turn, a ring for a turn on the spot); none for the Gyrocopter; none on the tick of a spawn, a respawn, a Swap or a bench, and none while destroyed or benched; the clear at R; the fade's arithmetic at 0 s, at the end of the hold, halfway and after the fade; the global uniforms set and reset; the data read back, with `first_problem()` refusing a negative width, a negative hold or fade, and an empty area; and a lint of the new files (the `mines_lint` way: `tools/evidence/split_screen/mines.gd:168`). `tyre_tracks_showcase` (windowed `--write-movie`, waiting for 120 fps) records each type driving a figure eight on the sand, then on the salt flat and the road, from the chase view and from above; a Truck through a ford (no tracks in the water, tracks again on the far bank); a Motorbike along a Base pad's edge; the same ground at 0 s, 30 s and 60 s; and two tracks crossing. `tyre_tracks_fps` (windowed) has both Units drive the busiest routes for a whole Round, A/B against the same run with `TYRE_TRACKS` off (AC-5).
- **Seeded defects.** At least: the jump step ignored (a spawn draws a line across the Map); the Unit's current contact used instead of the last tick's (a track ahead of the tyre: windowed only, record what the frames show); a stroke while not on the floor or destroyed; the Gyrocopter given contacts; additive strokes (crossings darker); `level` left out (tracks on the shore edge); the strength defaulting to 1 (tracks on a Map with no node); no clear at R; the fade counted on the wall clock (the pause fades them); a width written as a constant in code (the lint); the runner leaving the node on (the regression fails).
- **Docs in the same change** (`design/rules.md`, Process rules): in "Teams and visual style", the tracks as built (the texture's size and pixel, the look's values, the hold and the fade); `CONTEXT.md`'s "Tyre track" as built; README's How it plays; a Story 026 entry in `tests/smoke/critical-paths.md`; EPIC.md's row at the close; the ground shader's header (Purpose and Budget). A new `class_name` reaches the class cache only through `godot --headless --path . --import`.
