## Context

**GDD**: `design/game-brief.md`, `design/rules.md` ("Teams and visual style"; "Heights", for the Gyrocopter's drawn band)
**Requirement**: The developer's request of 2026-10-15, made in a session: "I want some sort of animation for the gyrocopter spinning blades. Something smooth. Blurred is fine, but it needs to be recognizable enough." No card of the author's board was named for it (the developer chose to draft the stories, 2026-10-15). The developer's answer of the same day (decided 2026-10-15): blades and a smear. The rotor turns at a steady rate and each of its two Team-coloured blades trails a soft see-through smear over about a quarter turn behind it, so the two blades can always be counted and the smear hides the step between frames; the pusher propeller gets the same look, its rate following the throttle. The two looks offered and not taken: a blur disc that replaces the blades while the rotor runs (you no longer see blades), and plain blades with no blur (they strobe at 60 fps at any rate that looks fast). Today the rotor stands still, yawed 25° and tilted 6° back as the concept kit's was (`tools/blender/build_gyrocopter.py:14`, `:177-192`).

## Acceptance Criteria

*From the developer's request and answer of 2026-10-15, scoped to this story:*

- [x] AC-1: While a Gyrocopter is in play its main rotor turns about its mast at a steady rate (data; 4 turns a second to start), in both views, from the tick it appears in its Garage until it leaves play (a destruction, a Swap or a bench hides the model with the Unit). In every frame its two blades can be told apart, in the Team colour with their darker tips: never a solid disc.
- [x] AC-2: Each blade trails a soft see-through smear over about a quarter turn behind it (data), strongest at the blade and fading to nothing, in the Unit's Team colour (an Orange Gyrocopter's smear reads orange, a Teal one's teal). The smear casts no shadow; the blades keep casting theirs, which now turn with them.
- [x] AC-3: The pusher propeller turns about its shaft with the same kind of smear, its rate following the Gyrocopter's speed: an idle rate while it stands, rising to a full rate at its top speed, forward or in reverse (both data).
- [x] AC-4: Smooth: the parts turn by the same step on every tick, with no jitter and no jump in play; no blade moves more than a set share of the gap between two blades in one 60 Hz tick (the strobe limit: the settings refuse a rate over it); and on a 120 Hz display the blades are drawn between ticks (physics interpolation), measured windowed at 120 fps.
- [x] AC-5: Nothing else moves: at angle 0 the rotor and the propeller stand exactly where the model has them today, and the mesh file's vertex boxes (`MeshUnitModel.hull_of()`, over every vertex and below `hull_top`) are unchanged bit for bit, so the badly damaged smoke, the explosion's centre and every height the evidence reads from the file stay. The band the parts sweep in a full turn is measured and recorded: the rotor's highest drawn point (1.802 m in the file's frame at rest today) stays under the water towers' platforms, and the blades and the propeller clear the model's frame in every position. *(Accepted by the developer on 2026-10-16 with this last clause missed by 12.0 mm at one position: the propeller's lowest tip passes 12.0 mm into the thin mast brace; see question 6, TD-018 and the Completion Notes.)*
- [x] AC-6: Every value is data: the rotor's rate, the propeller's idle and full rates, the smear's arc and strength, the hub and axis of each turning part and the strobe share; no number in code but 0 and 1.
- [x] AC-7: Two Gyrocopters in both views keep the project budget, measured windowed at 1280 x 720 the `map_fps` way: the lowest one-second rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms and at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together. The draw calls the turning parts and smears add, and the frame of a Gyrocopter's first appearance, are recorded as observations beside TD-016 and TD-017, never as thresholds.
- [x] AC-8: The older evidence scenarios keep their outputs byte for byte against the capture taken at the start of the story: every scenario that does not declare the new opt-in sees today's Gyrocopter model, drawn from a byte copy of today's mesh file under `tools/evidence/frozen/`, with nothing turning. The kit fallback scene (`gyrocopter_kit_model.tscn`) is not changed.

## Implementation Notes

*At `minimal` there is no ADR; these are pointers, not decisions. The file:line pointers are `main` at 14eec71.*

- **What 14eec71 gives.** The Gyrocopter's model is one mesh file, `assets/art/vehicles/wasteland_gyrocopter/wasteland_gyrocopter.glb`, holding three meshes (Accent, Neutral, Lamp) that `finish()` joins by palette key (`tools/blender/wasteland_kit.py:555`; the default `groups` at `:574`). The rotor's blades and tips (`build_gyrocopter.py:177-192`) are part of Accent and its hub of Neutral; the propeller's blades and tips (`:137-143`) are part of Neutral and Accent. Nothing can turn on its own. `MeshUnitModel._build_meshes()` (`src/gameplay/units/models/mesh_unit_model.gd:60`) adds one MeshInstance3D per mesh with `UnitModel._add_mesh()` (`unit_model.gd:309`). The Accent child is in `Unit.TEAM_COLOUR_GROUP`, painted by `Unit._paint_team_colour()` (`unit.gd:888`), which searches depth first, so a Team mesh under a pivot is painted too. The model scene (`gyrocopter_mesh_model.tscn`) stands it at `hover_height` 2.5 m with `hull_top` 1.4, the rotor above that. Physics interpolation is on (`project.godot`, `physics/common/physics_interpolation`) and World turns it ON for its children (`src/gameplay/split_screen/split_screen.gd:58-62`), so a node turned in its physics tick is drawn between ticks.
- **The mesh file** (rebuilt with the command in `tools/blender/readme.md`). `build_gyrocopter.py` passes `finish()` explicit `groups`: Accent, Neutral and Lamp as today minus the turning parts, plus four objects, say RotorAccent (the blades and tips), RotorNeutral (the hub; the rotor head under it can stay on the mast), PropellerAccent (the tips) and PropellerNeutral (the blades, the hub and the spinner); the names are the build's call. Every object stays in the model's frame at its rest pose (no `origins`), so each vertex of the file stays where it is today, and `MeshUnitModel.hull_of()` (`mesh_unit_model.gd:85`), which reads every mesh of a file, returns the same boxes: the reads at `tools/evidence/split_screen/heights_scan.gd:138-139`, `heights_kit.gd:99`, `:129`, `:150` and `turret_looks_growth.gd:93` stay. Measure first: `hull_of(file, INF)` and `hull_of(file, 1.4)` of the rebuilt file against today's, to the bit. The occlusion bake takes the same occluders (every joined object but Lamp: `wasteland_kit.py:580`), so compare the body meshes' vertex colours against today's file and record any change. The other three Units' files are not rebuilt.
- **The axes.** Blender's +Y forward is Godot's -Z, so a Blender point (x, y, z) is (x, z, -y) in the model's frame. The rotor's hub (`ROTOR_HUB`, `build_gyrocopter.py:37`) is at (0, 1.58, 0.1). Its axis is the mast's: Blender's up tilted back by `ROTOR_TILT`, 6° about X (`:42`, `:192`), which is (0, cos 6°, sin 6°) = (0, 0.9945, 0.1045), with the blades coned 2.5° about it (`:40`). The propeller's hub is at (0, 0.9, 0.63) (`ENGINE`'s height and `PROP_Y`: `:44-45`) and it turns about Z. Store each part's hub and axis as data, on the model scene or in the settings below, and check them against the meshes (the hub box's centre, the blades' plane).
- **The turning** (in MeshUnitModel, or a small node class of its own: the build's call). For each turning part the model adds a pivot Node3D at the hub, with the part's MeshInstance3D as its child at minus the hub (so its vertices stay in the file's frame), and turns the pivot about the axis by its rate on each physics tick. The propeller's rate is `lerp(idle, full, share)`, where the share is the parent Unit's real speed along its facing (`CharacterBody3D.get_real_velocity()`) over its `stats.max_speed`, read from the parent, so `unit.gd` is not edited. A model whose file has no turning meshes (the frozen copy, below, and the other three types) adds no pivot and draws as today. Measure first, windowed at 120 fps: that a pivot turned in `_physics_process` under World's interpolation is drawn between ticks, and with no streak on its first frame. Story 016's lesson applies: `reset_physics_interpolation()` on the tick the model enters the tree (`story-016-unit-explosions.md:49`).
- **The smear.** A flat disc (or a ring) in each part's plane, a child of its pivot so that it turns with the blades, drawn by a ShaderMaterial of a new spatial shader (say `src/gameplay/units/models/spatial_unit_rotor_smear.gdshader`). For a point at angle θ about the hub and a blade at angle β, the angle behind the blade is (β − θ) wrapped into one blade gap in the turning direction; the alpha falls from the smear's strength at the blade to 0 at the smear's arc (about 90° to start, AC-2), with a darker tip band as the blades have. The material is transparent, draws both faces, writes no depth and casts no shadow. The Team colour: the smear cannot take the Unit's `team_material`, a StandardMaterial3D, so the model reads the painted Accent's colour once the Unit has painted it and sets it as a per-instance uniform (`set_instance_shader_parameter()`: scalars and vectors only, at most 16 per shader, per the 4.7 shading-language reference). The paint runs right after the model enters the tree (`unit.gd:865-882`), so the model's first physics tick sees it. No material is duplicated per Unit and `unit.gd` is not edited. Measure first that the Team material's albedo is the Team colour (`design/rules.md`, "Teams and visual style": each is the albedo of `team_orange_material.tres` / `team_teal_material.tres`).
- **The strobe limit** (AC-4). The step per 60 Hz tick is rate × 360° / 60: 24° for the rotor's 4 turns a second against a blade gap of 180°, and 12° to 30° for the propeller's 2 to 5 turns a second against 120° (starting values, judged in frames). A settings Resource (say `RotorSettings` in `src/gameplay/units/data/gyrocopter_rotor_settings.tres`, with zero defaults and a `first_problem()` like ExplosionSettings) refuses a rate whose step is more than its strobe share (0.35 to start) of the blade gap, a negative or NaN value, and an arc outside 0 to one gap.
- **The swept band** (AC-5). Turning shows what the rest pose hides. With the disc tilted 6° back and the blades coned 2.5°, the tips reach about 1.92 m up at the front and 1.44 m at the back in the file's frame (from the script's numbers: 1.58 m ± 2.3 m × sin(6° ± 2.5°)), against 1.802 m at rest (`design/rules.md`, "Heights": the drawn band 2.5000 to 4.3016 m). A full turn also sweeps a 2.3 m circle, which reaches farther into a water tower's legs than the 0.416 m recorded at rest (same paragraph). Measure the band over a full turn and the sweep against the legs, and record both in "Heights" as built. Check the propeller's sweep against the keel (about 3 cm under its lowest tip by the script's numbers: `build_gyrocopter.py:32-34`, `:44-47`) and the rotor's against the fin and the propeller.
- **The Round-over and the states.** The tree pauses at the end of the tick that ends a Round (`story-016-unit-explosions.md:54`), so the rotor stops under the Round-over screen along with everything else that counts ticks (a reading, asked below). `destroy()` and `leave_play()` hide the Unit with its model (`unit.gd:589`, `:619`) and `spawn()` shows it (`:671`), so there is nothing to stop or start. Whether the angle restarts at 0 at each spawn (for reproducible frames) is the build's call.
- **The older outputs** (AC-8). The evidence scenarios measured today's Gyrocopter. `drawn_box()` (`heights_kit.gd:169`) merges the world boxes of every visible GeometryInstance3D under a model each tick, so a rotor that turns, or that is drawn by a mesh of its own, changes the printed boxes even at angle 0 (the yawed box of a merge is not the merge of the yawed boxes). The runner (`tools/evidence/split_screen_harness.gd`) therefore gets a new opt-in, `const ROTORS: bool = true`, which this story's scenarios declare, read from the script's constant map as `HEIGHTS` and `EXPLOSIONS` are. For every other scenario the Gyrocopter draws a byte copy of today's mesh file (`tools/evidence/frozen/wasteland_gyrocopter_story_024.glb`, from `git show 14eec71:<path>`, its `.import` written by the import), which has no turning meshes. Both scenes the runner gives the Gyrocopter name the shipped file (`gyrocopter_mesh_model.tscn:4` and the frozen `gyrocopter_model_story_019.tscn:4`), and a frozen scene is never edited (`tools/evidence/frozen/readme.md`), so the runner sets `mesh_scene` on the model it gives those scenarios (for instance by packing an instance that names the frozen file: the build's call), recorded in the frozen readme's table. Measure first by running the older scenarios against the rebuilt file with the copy in place. The baseline is the 75 retained outputs of story 024's regression (`production/qa/evidence/story-024-ammunition-buildings-evidence.md`), captured twice before the first edit; grep every retained output for `\.gd:[0-9]+` and plan an exact rewrite in `measurements/normalizers.json` for each line this story moves (the runner's own, and `mesh_unit_model.gd` and `unit_model.gd` if an output prints them).
- **The evidence scenarios.** `rotors` (headless, run twice byte-identical, declaring `ROTORS` and the opt-ins `heights` declares) checks: the pivots' angles on each tick against rate × ticks; the propeller's rate standing, at half speed, at full speed and in reverse; nothing drawn before the spawn or after a destruction; the turning meshes painted with the Team's material and the smear's instance colour equal to the Team's albedo, for both Players; no shadow and no collider on the smear; the file's boxes against today's; the band over a full turn and the sweep against the water towers' legs (AC-5's numbers); the explosion's centre and the smoke's place unchanged at a destruction; the settings read back, with `first_problem()` refusing a rate over the strobe limit, a negative rate and an arc wider than a gap; and a lint of the new files (no number but 0 and 1, the `mines_lint` way: `tools/evidence/split_screen/mines.gd:168`). `rotors_showcase` (windowed `--write-movie`, the `explosions_showcase` pattern, waiting for 120 fps) records each Player's Gyrocopter standing and at full speed, in the chase view and from above, Orange and Teal, plus four consecutive frames of one rotor to show the turn and the smear. `rotors_fps` (windowed) runs two Gyrocopters in both views (AC-7), A/B against the same run with `ROTORS` off.
