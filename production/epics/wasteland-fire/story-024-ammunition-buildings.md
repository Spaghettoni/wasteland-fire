## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 8 and the developer's answers of 2026-10-10, scoped to this story:*

- [ ] AC-1: One ammunition building stands on each half of Map 01, mirrored across x = 0, near the old salt-flat Fuel Can spots (about x -62 and 62, z 14), with its ammunition zone right in front of its side that faces the centre. It is low, no taller than the 2 m Wall Panels: the Gyrocopter flies over it at its flying height with no stop and no crash, while ground Units and every Shot stop at it like a piece of cover (a ground Unit's, a Turret's, and the Gyrocopter's fired from above it: A2), and like cover it blocks a Turret's sight. At its places on Map 01 nothing behind it is within a Turret's reach but a sliver too narrow for any Unit (computed from the building's footprint and Base A's Turrets at world (-101, +-10), whose Shots reach 36.6 m from their centre), so in play it hides nothing from the Turrets and only Unit Shots and a Turret's missed Shots meet it; the sight is checked with a sight-ray query from a Base Turret's muzzle through the building to a point behind it, on the Shot mask plus `sight_extra_mask` (`src/gameplay/defences/turret.gd:253-256`). Nothing damages or destroys it, and it is not Cover. Each ammunition zone is drawn on the ground so a Player sees where to stop, in both Players' views and in both camera views, and the ammunition buildings and their zones are told apart from the Fuel building and its zones by more than colour.
- [ ] AC-2: A Unit that has stopped in an ammunition zone rearms gradually: ammo only, from empty to a full load in about 4 s for every type (a whole Shot at a time: 100 Shots for a Motorbike, 50 for a Buggy, 25 for a Truck, 40 for a Gyrocopter), up to full and no further, its Ammo line rising as it fills. Stopped is story 023's test (R13): a Unit held against the building with the throttle on rearms, one rolling through at any speed does not, and one that moves off or leaves the zone stops rearming within the measured latency; whether a parked Unit that another Unit drives into rearms is measured and recorded, with no outcome fixed (a CharacterBody3D is moved only by its own `move_and_slide()`). Units of both Players rearm at the same zone on the same ticks and at either building (no owner), a zone never runs dry and holds nothing R must restore, and an ammunition zone gives no Fuel, no Mines (R3) and no hit points, while a Fuel zone still gives Fuel only.
- [ ] AC-3: A Gyrocopter hovering still over an ammunition zone at its flying height rearms as a ground Unit does; it reaches each zone from its own Base's side, over the ammunition building, without touching a Tall piece.
- [ ] AC-4: A Mine lay is refused where any part of the Mine would lie in a supply zone or within about 3 m of one, at all four zones (story 023's two Fuel zones and the two ammunition zones), for both Players: the press lays nothing, keeps the count, and that Player's notice line, and only that Player's, shows "No Mines at a supply building" for about 1.5 s, as a refused lay on a Gate approach does. Everywhere else the rules of story 014 hold: the two canyon spots and the depot's five old Fuel Can spots are open ground like the ground around them, while the two salt-flat spots now lie on an ammunition zone's edge and are refused.
- [ ] AC-5: Every value is data: the ammo's time from empty to full (4 s) in the SupplySettings `.tres`; the building's size, collider and look in its scene; each zone's size, height and kind in its scene; the buildings' places in `map_01.tscn`; the margin around a zone in `mine_settings.tres`; the notice text in `player_notice.tscn`, through `tr()`. No number is a constant in code. The evidence scenarios written before this story run on the Map they were measured on (no ammunition building, no ammunition zone, no lay refused at a zone) and keep their outputs byte for byte against the baseline captured at the start of the story, but for exact rewrites pinned in `normalizers.json`; each strip is proven by a seeded "runner leaves it on" defect. The frame rate with both buildings and all four zones drawn stays within the project budget: the lowest one-second rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms, at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together.
