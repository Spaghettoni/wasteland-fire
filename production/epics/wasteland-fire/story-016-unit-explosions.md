## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 6, the developer's answers and the reading of 2026-10-10, scoped to this story:*

- [ ] AC-1: Every destruction the Round counts plays one explosion, on the tick it happens: a Unit destroyed by the other Player's Shots, by a Turret's Shot, by a Mine, by its own Self-destruct (the key outside the own Base) or, for the Gyrocopter, by running out of Fuel; and any later way of destroying a Unit that ends in `Unit.destroy()` plays it with no change to the explosion (story 021's crash into a Tall piece; shown here by a tool's direct `destroy()` of a Gyrocopter in play). Every type explodes the same way whatever the cause, but for its size. One explosion per destroyed Unit: a Mine that takes two Units plays two, and a Unit that a Mine and a Shot take on one tick plays one.
- [ ] AC-2: Nothing else plays one: not a Swap at the own Base, the benches at the Round's start and at R, a respawn, a second hit on a Unit already destroyed, or a Turret or a Flag Wall that falls. And the explosion changes nothing the Round does: with explosions playing, the Token is taken and a carried Flag dropped at the wreck on the destroying tick, the Unit respawns on the tick the respawn delay and its Player's choice give, a destroyed last Motorbike ends the Round on its tick and two on one tick are still the double loss.
- [ ] AC-3: It is a picture only: it holds no physics body, no Area3D and no collision layer or mask, so it damages, pushes and stops nothing (a Unit drives through it, a Shot fired through it hits what lies behind, a Motorbike picks up the Flag dropped in it), leaves no wreck and frees itself about 2 s after it began, before the destroyed Unit can respawn. R removes every explosion still playing.
- [ ] AC-4: It is centred on the destroyed Unit as it was drawn on the destroying tick, never on the body's origin on the floor: a ground Unit's at the middle of its box where it died, a Gyrocopter's at the height its model was drawn (hovering, or risen over a piece of cover or a cliff), so a Gyrocopter shot down over the canyon rock or a piece of cover explodes above it, where it was seen, not inside it. It stays where it began: it does not follow the Unit to its Garage at the respawn and does not move with either camera.
- [ ] AC-5: The look: a short flash, a fireball that swells and then shrinks and darkens, a few dark chunks thrown up and out that fall back, and a little grey smoke that rises, grows and fades, all from Godot's built-in meshes, flat-shaded in neutral fire, steel and smoke colours, with no Team colour and no shadow, and sized from the destroyed type's box, so a Truck's is larger than a Motorbike's. Both views draw every explosion, in the view from above and in the chase view, at its place from its first frame and moving smoothly at 120 fps; the smoke stays thin enough that a Flag dropped at the wreck reads through it, and the fireball does not read as Team Orange.
- [ ] AC-6: A Mine kill shows both pictures on the same tick: the Mine's own 0.3 s flash, as kept on 2026-10-09, and the destroyed Unit's explosion. The Mine, its flash and its timing are unchanged.
- [ ] AC-7 (reading, 2026-10-10; `design/rules.md`, "Destruction and respawn"): The explosion of the destruction that ends a Round keeps playing under the Round-over screen instead of standing still on its first picture, and R removes it if it still plays; it destroys nothing, so the winner, the double loss and the stopped Round are as before.
- [ ] AC-8: Three explosions at once, each drawn in both views, keep the frame within the project budget, measured windowed at 1280 x 720 the `map_fps` way: the lowest one-second rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms and at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together (`.claude/docs/technical-preferences.md:41-43`; story 013 AC-8); the frame of a Round's first explosion stays within 16.6 ms too. The measured rate and draw calls are recorded as observations beside story 012's 222 and 223 (`production/qa/evidence/story-012-flag-walls-evidence.md:20`) and story 013's windowed `turrets_fps` run, never as thresholds.
- [ ] AC-9: Every value is data: the durations, the size factors, the number of chunks with their fixed directions and speeds, the gravity they fall under, the smoke's puffs, rise and growth, and every colour in an ExplosionSettings `.tres`; the meshes and materials in `explosion.tscn`; the scene itself in `split_screen.tscn`. No number is a constant in code and no direction is random, so every run is the same. The evidence scenarios written before this story, every one that does not declare `EXPLOSIONS`, run with no explosions (no Explosion is ever added and nothing new listens to the Round) and keep their outputs byte for byte against the capture taken at the start of the story, but for exact rewrites pinned in `normalizers.json` (the runner's lines their backtraces print, which the new entries move); `unit.gd`, `match_controller.gd`, `player_match_input.gd`, `mine.gd` and `mine.tscn` are not edited.

*At `minimal` there is no ADR; these are pointers, not decisions. The file:line pointers are `main` at 73de824, before story 015 edits the Turrets and the runner: re-find them as story 015 leaves them.*

- **What 73de824 gives (AC-1, AC-2).** Every destruction ends in `Unit.destroy()` (`src/gameplay/units/unit.gd:484-503`): the Unit leaves play, hidden (`:496`), its layers zeroed (`:497-498`) and its tick off (`:499`), then emits `destroyed` once (`:503`). The lethal hit of `apply_damage()` takes the same path (`:458-462`), so the developer's debug-damage key, which calls its own Unit (`src/gameplay/split_screen/split_screen.gd:67-68`), explodes too; an empty Gyrocopter calls `destroy()` first thing in its own tick (`unit.gd:394-396`, `:681-682`). A Shot's kill lands in the Shot's tick and a Self-destruct in PlayerMatchInput's (`src/gameplay/match/match_controller.gd:100-103`, `:404-410`); a Turret's kill is its Shot's (story 013) and a Mine's is made in the Mine's own tick (`src/gameplay/mines/mine.gd:187`; `story-014-truck-mines.md:52`). `destroyed` reaches `MatchController._on_unit_destroyed()` synchronously (`match_controller.gd:102-103`), which refuses a Player who is not ALIVE (`src/gameplay/match/garage_queue.gd:286-289`), drops a carried Flag, takes the Token and emits `flag_dropped` and then `unit_destroyed(player_index)` (`match_controller.gd:723-731`). A Swap and every bench go through `GarageQueue.bench()` and `Unit.leave_play()` (`garage_queue.gd:221-231`; `unit.gd:514-526`), which never emits `destroyed` (`unit.gd:510-511`), and `_apply_swaps()` emits `unit_swapped` alone (`match_controller.gd:573-580`). So one listener on `MatchController.unit_destroyed` (`:144`) hears every counted destruction and nothing else: AC-1 and AC-2 follow from where it listens, not from a list of causes, and no signal needs to say why the Unit died.
- **The hook.** In SplitScreen, the composition root that already wires the Shots, the Turrets and the Mines (`split_screen.gd:92-125`, `:178-195`): an export `explosion_scene`, set in `split_screen.tscn`, and in `_ready()`, after `begin()` and beside the four `round_started` connections (`:192-195`), `match_controller.unit_destroyed` connected to a handler that instances the scene, adds it under World, the Units' parent (`unit.get_parent()`, as `_arm_turrets()` hands the Turrets theirs: `:242`), places it and starts it, all in that one call. SplitScreen connects the handler only while `explosion_scene` is set (documented: no scene, no explosions), so the runner's strip is one assignment (below), and `explosions` checks that the shipped scene sets it. Never a link on `Unit.destroyed`: `tokens_data` prints that signal's links (`tools/evidence/split_screen/tokens_data_kit.gd:113-115`, `:200-203`) and new code never listens to it (`src/gameplay/defences/turret.gd:21-22`); never a call from `Unit.destroy()`, which calls nobody (`unit.gd:121-122`); never a child of the Unit, which `destroy()` hides at once (`unit.gd:496`) and `spawn()` carries to its Base (`unit.gd:563`). No retained output prints the links of `unit_destroyed` (the runner connects to it for the legacy scenarios and prints its emits: `tools/evidence/split_screen_harness.gd:83`, `:529-530`; `mines_doubles` counts a Weapon's `fired` links: `tools/evidence/split_screen/mines_doubles.gd:174`), and the strip keeps the older scenarios off it anyway.
- **The handler runs inside another node's tick** (`match_controller.gd:100-103`; never a physics signal handler: `unit.gd:190-194`), so adding a node there is safe, as a Weapon adds its Shot and a MineLayer its Mine. Place the explosion and call `reset_physics_interpolation()` on the tick it enters the tree, as `Shot.launch()` does (`src/gameplay/combat/shot.gd:15-18`) and the Mine does (`mine.gd:167`): World draws its children interpolated (`split_screen.gd:58-62`), and a node moved after that tick is drawn streaking from its old place on its first frame, which only a windowed run at 120 fps shows.
- **Where (AC-4).** The centre is the destroyed Unit's model, its child named `Model` (`unit.gd:220`, `:743-745`), read in the handler with `get_node_or_null(Unit.MODEL_NODE_NAME)`, so `unit.gd` needs no accessor: the destroyed Unit stays in the tree where it died until its respawn (`unit.gd:116-120`), its model with it. Raise it by the height of the type's `collision_center` (0.5 m for the Motorbike and the Gyrocopter, 0.55 m for the Buggy, 0.8 m for the Truck: `src/gameplay/units/data/motorbike_stats.tres:30`, `gyrocopter_stats.tres:29`, `buggy_stats.tres:28`, `truck_stats.tres:28`), and take the Unit's own origin when it has no model. The Gyrocopter's model stands `hover_height` 0.78 m up plus its rise over the cover and the cliffs (`src/gameplay/units/models/unit_model.gd:151-166`, `:229-230`; `gyrocopter_mesh_model.tscn:10-15`), so its blast is where it was seen; story 020 moves that model to one constant flying height, about 2.5 m (the Truck's pod plus a clearance; its thin antenna is not counted; measured in story 015, set in story 020), and turns its rise off, and the same rule then puts the blast there with no change here. The model is the Unit's child and ticks after it, so in a Shot's or a Mine's tick it already stands at this frame's rise and in the Unit's own Fuel-crash tick at the last frame's, at most one tick's rise apart (the rise rate is 6 m/s: `gyrocopter_mesh_model.tscn:15`); its own tick keeps running after `destroy()` stops the Unit's (`unit.gd:499`; `unit_model.gd:140`), which is harmless, since the handler reads it once.
- **The Explosion** (`src/gameplay/effects/explosion.gd` and `explosion.tscn`, `class_name Explosion`; the folder is the build's call): a Node3D whose root is in the node group `"explosions"`, stored in the scene as `mine.tscn:25` stores `"mines"`, with no CollisionObject3D anywhere under it (AC-3).
