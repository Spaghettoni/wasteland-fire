## Acceptance Criteria

*From the author's comment 3, the developer's answers A1 and A10 and the readings R10 and R18 of 2026-10-10, scoped to the walls of both Garages and the two scrap walls in the field:*

- [ ] AC-1: Each Garage's three walls are Wall Panels like story 018's: 4 along the back and 3 along each side, 10 per Garage and 20 on the Map, each 2 m tall (the walls they replace were 2.5 m) and 1 m thick as before, fitted end to end and flush, with no gap and no overlap, in the old walls' footprint (Base-local: the back from x -7 to 7 at z 12 to 13, each panel 3.5 m long; each side from z 2 to 12 at |x| 6 to 7, each 3.333 m), mirror images across x = 0, dark like the Base walls. They belong to their Base's Player (R10): 40 hit points, broken by the other Player's Buggy (4 Shots), Truck (2) or Gyrocopter (3) and never by a Motorbike, however long it fires; a Player's own Shots of every type end on that Player's own Garage's panels and take nothing; they show the Flag Walls' two damaged looks and fall into low rubble at 0, as story 018 AC-3 and AC-4 say. The Garage floor, its Marking and its spawn spots (Base-local (±4, 0, 5)) stay where they were: every new Unit still appears on its spot, the test for a free spawn spot ignores the panels, a Mine's lay in the Garage is still refused with "No Mines in or near a Base", standing panels or not, and from either spot every type still drives straight out through the Gate on the throttle alone.
- [ ] AC-2: A standing Garage panel stops ground Units, flush at its face from the Garage and from the yard, and every Shot, as a Base wall's panel does. The Gyrocopter flies over the Garage walls, so it may leave or enter its Garage over any of them; the corner towers and the water tower's legs still stop it. How high the Gyrocopter's model is drawn while it stands on each spawn spot, beside a Garage wall its rise now sees, is measured on the spawn tick and over the next second and retained in a frame (story 020 fixes one flying height).
- [ ] AC-3: A broken Garage wall exposes its Garage. With the side panel beside a spawn spot down, the other Player's Unit in the yard beside the Garage hits a Unit standing on that spot through the breach (its Shot ends on that Unit and takes its damage off it), and every ground type drives into or out of the Garage through it, the 2.4 m Truck through the 3.333 m gap; with that panel standing, the same Shot ends on the panel. A back panel down opens only onto the 0.7 m gap between the Garage and the Base's back wall (Base-local z 13.0 to 13.7), where no Unit fits (the narrowest, the Motorbike, is 1.4 m wide), so it changes nothing while the back wall's panel behind it stands; with both down, a Shot from outside the Base reaches the Garage through the two breaches.
- [ ] AC-4: The two scrap walls in the field become Wall Panels: 4 each, 3 m long, 2 m tall and 2.5 m thick like the scrap walls they replace, flush end to end in their footprint (world x -13.25 to -10.75 and 10.75 to 13.25, z 6 to 18), mirror images across x = 0, in the Flag Walls' look (question 3). They belong to no Player (R10): the Shots of both Players damage them by the matrix, so a panel falls to the 4th Buggy Shot, the 2nd Truck Shot or the 3rd Gyrocopter Shot of either Player, and a Motorbike's Shot ends on one and takes nothing. While one stands, ground Units and every Shot stop at it, the Gyrocopter flies over it (its model rising as over the cover until story 020), and a lay within a Mine's reach of it is refused with "No room for a Mine here"; once it has fallen, the ground is open: Units drive through the gap, Shots fly through it and a Truck lays a Mine in its footprint.
- [ ] AC-5: The scrap walls' panels are listed on the Map, not in a Base: Map 01's Map holds the list of its Structures that belong to no Base, SplitScreen leaves their Player at none (-1) and restores them at every restart with the Bases' pieces, from that list and with no search of the tree. They are no longer Cover (R10): Cover is now the eight wrecks and the two containers, which still stand all Round, take no damage (a Shot ends on one and nothing about it changes) and stop what they stopped, with the Gyrocopter still flying over them.
- [ ] AC-6: A fallen Garage or field panel stays down for the rest of the Round: no key or rule builds, repairs or captures one, and no destruction, respawn, Swap or Flag drop brings it back. R brings back every panel of both Garages and of the field on the restart, with 40 hit points, solid, its whole look shown and the rubble gone, after both Units are benched, so no panel closes on a Unit (checked with a Unit parked in a Garage breach and one in a field breach when R is pressed). Each Base then lists 46 Structures (its 4 Flag Walls, its 2 Turrets, the 30 Wall Panels of its walls and the 10 of its Garage) and the Map 8.
- [ ] AC-7: Retained windowed frames show both Garages whole, a Garage panel at each damaged look, a Garage side breach with a Unit on the spot behind it, both field walls whole and one breached, in the chase view and in the view from above (the list is under Test Evidence). `map_fps` and `turrets_fps`, run windowed with the 88 new Wall Panels (96 with the Flag Walls) in place, in each of three states (every panel whole; every panel at its second damaged look; every panel fallen), stay within the project's budget as story 018 AC-8 sets it (`.claude/docs/technical-preferences.md:41-43`; story 013 AC-8): the lowest one-second frame rate at least 60 fps, a frame at most 16.6 ms, and at most 1,000 draw calls for both views together, the provisional budget.
